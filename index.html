Guidelines for the management of atrial fibrillation in adult patients admitted to an intensive care unit.

Ann Intensive Care·2026-10-19·PMID 42894798
房颤与消融 Journal Article

Vincent Labbé、Nadia Aissaoui、Pierre Asfar 等 14 位作者

英文摘要

Most existing recommendations for atrial fibrillation are derived from cardiology settings. However, atrial fibrillation is also highly prevalent among patients admitted to intensive care units and is associated with adverse clinical outcomes. Therefore, its management in this specific context warrants dedicated guidance. To address this need, The French Intensive Care Society has developed formalized expert recommendations using the Grading of Recommendations Assessment, Development, and Evaluation (GRADE) methodology. Twenty-four questions addressing the management of atrial fibrillation were evaluated in adult patients admitted to intensive care units. Of these, twenty-one focused specifically on new-onset atrial fibrillation, one addressed pre-existing atrial fibrillation, and two were applicable to both conditions. The assessment encompassed the role of transthoracic and transesophageal echocardiography, the management of potentially reversible triggers (optimization of fluid status, magnesium supplementation, serum potassium targets, and screening for thyroid dysfunction…), strategies for mitigating hemodynamic risk (indications and modalities for rhythm and rate control), and the management of thrombotic risk (indications, regimens, and duration of therapeutic anticoagulation). Eighteen recommendations were formulated, all of which achieved strong agreement among the expert panel. Only two recommendations were supported by a low level of evidence (GRADE 2+), whereas the remaining sixteen relied on evidence that could not be classified within the GRADE framework and were therefore issued as expert opinions. For six additional aspects of atrial fibrillation management, the experts concluded that the available evidence was insufficient to support the formulation of a reliable recommendation.

研究动机

现行房颤指南多来自心内科人群，而 ICU 住院患者房颤发生率同样很高，且与不良临床结局相关，这一特定情境下的管理（尤其是新发房颤）缺乏针对性指导，需要专门建议来填补证据缺口。

方法

法国重症医学会采用 GRADE 方法制定正式专家建议。围绕 ICU 成人患者的房颤管理提出 24 个问题，其中 21 个专门针对新发房颤、1 个针对既往房颤、2 个两者通用。评估内容包括：经胸与经食管超声心动图的作用、潜在可逆诱因的处理（容量状态优化、补镁、血钾目标、甲状腺功能筛查等）、血流动力学风险消减策略（节律控制与心率控制的指征和方式）、血栓风险管理（治疗性抗凝的指征、方案和疗程）。摘要未报告文献检索与证据分级的统计方法细节。

结果

最终形成 18 条建议，全部获得专家组强烈一致同意。证据水平方面，仅 2 条建议基于低级别证据（GRADE 2+），其余 16 条所依据的证据无法纳入 GRADE 框架，故以专家意见形式发布。另有 6 个房颤管理方面，专家认为现有证据不足，无法形成可靠建议。摘要未报告各条建议的具体内容、一致率数值及具体终点数据。

结论与意义

该文件提示，ICU 房颤（尤其是新发房颤）管理领域的高质量证据仍十分匮乏，绝大多数推荐属于专家共识而非循证推荐，临床实践中应结合个体血流动力学状态、可逆诱因纠正与抗凝风险权衡来执行，并应以此为契机推动针对 ICU 人群的前瞻性研究。

Echocardiography-derived Left Atrial Volumetric-Mechanical Coupling Index predicts early new-onset atrial fibrillation after embolic stroke/transient ischemic attack of undetermined source.

Clin Res Cardiol·2026-10-19·PMID 42895543
房颤与消融 Journal Article

Chiara Giordano、Giacomo Gamberini、Andrea Faggiano 等 17 位作者

英文摘要

AIM: In patients with embolic stroke or transient ischemic attack of undetermined source (ESUS/TIA), occult atrial fibrillation (AF) is a frequent underlying mechanism with critical therapeutic implications, yet selecting patients who warrant intensified rhythm monitoring remains challenging. The Left Atrioventricular Coupling Index (LACI = LA end-diastolic volume/LV end-diastolic volume) captures atrioventricular uncoupling, whereas the Left Atrial Volumetric-Mechanical Coupling Index (LACI-VM = LAVi/a') integrates atrial size with mechanical performance; both may capture complementary domains of atrial remodelling relevant to subclinical AF. We aimed to investigate the association of LACI-VM and LACI with incident AF in ESUS/TIA. METHODS AND RESULTS: In this retrospective single-center cohort, 285 consecutive patients with ESUS/TIA of undetermined source underwent transthoracic echocardiography at index hospitalization, including measurement of LACI-VM and LACI. The primary endpoint was new-onset AF during follow-up. Median age was 71 years and 43% were women. Over a median follow-up of 10.9 months, AF occurred in 24 patients (8.4%), with 62% of events occurring within the first year. LACI-VM was higher in patients who developed AF and remained independently associated with incident AF after multivariable adjustment (HR 6.64, CI 1.34-59.88; p = 0.041). LACI showed a non-significant association overall (HR 2.28, CI 0.74-7.01; p = 0.09), with predictive value confined to events within 12 months. A combined assessment of LACI-VM and LV filling pressures further refined risk stratification: patients with preserved LACI-VM (< 2.5) and normal filling pressures (E/e' ≤ 10) had a very low AF incidence (~ 2%), identifying a truly low-risk subgroup; conversely, when both LACI-VM (≥ 2.5) and filling pressures (E/e' > 10) were impaired, AF risk was ~ tenfold higher (~ 20%). CONCLUSION: In ESUS/TIA, echocardiography-derived LACI-VM independently predicts incident AF and identifies patients at genuinely low risk. As a simple echocardiographic metric, its integration with LV filling pressures may support a more personalized use of rhythm monitoring.

结论与意义

在ESUS/TIA患者中，超声心动图衍生的LACI-VM可独立预测新发房颤并识别真正低危患者；作为简易超声指标，其与左室充盈压整合有助于更个体化地选择心律监测策略。局限性包括回顾性单中心设计、房颤事件数较少及随访时间有限，结论需外部验证。

Determinants of outcome in atrial functional tricuspid regurgitation.

Echo Res Pract·2026-10-19·PMID 42894198
房颤与消融 Journal Article

Jawza Aldakhil、Kush Patel、Sveeta Badiani 等 6 位作者

英文摘要

BACKGROUND: Atrial functional tricuspid regurgitation (AFTR) is increasingly recognized as a result of atrial remodelling in patients with atrial fibrillation (AF). However, prognostic predictors in this population remain incompletely defined. OBJECTIVES: To evaluate echocardiographic characteristics and identify predictors of all-cause mortality in patients with AFTR. METHODS: We retrospectively studied consecutive patients with AF who underwent transthoracic echocardiography between 2017 and 2025. Inclusion required significant right atrial remodelling, defined as a right atrial area > 25 cm2. Patients with at least moderate functional tricuspid regurgitation comprised the AFTR cohort, while patients with mild/trivial/no tricuspid regurgitation served as controls. Patients with primary valvular disease, cardiomyopathy, congenital heart disease, device-related valve disease, RV dilatation, or more than mild RV systolic dysfunction were excluded. Cox proportional hazards regression was performed to identify predictors of mortality. RESULTS: A total of 152 patients were included, comprising 120 patients with AFTR and 32 controls. Compared with controls, patients with AFTR demonstrated larger atrial dimensions, higher right-sided hemodynamic-burden, impaired RV longitudinal strain, and reduced reservoir RA strain. During a median follow-up of 6.3 years, 53 deaths (45%) occurred among patients with AFTR. In univariable Cox analysis, age (HR 1.02, 95% CI 1.00-1.04; p = 0.016), TR Vmax (HR 1.59, 95% CI 1.07-2.35; p = 0.020), and lower reservoir RA strain (HR 0.90, 95% CI 0.84-0.96; p = 0.003) were associated with mortality. In multivariable analysis, reservoir RA strain remained independently associated with mortality (adjusted HR 0.88, 95% CI 0.81-0.95; p < 0.001). CONCLUSION: AFTR is characterized by marked biatrial remodelling and impaired right atrial function. Reduced RA reservoir strain was independently associated with mortality, supporting a potential role for RA deformation imaging in risk stratification in AFTR.

Distinct hemodynamic implications of junctional rhythm versus sinus rhythm on cardiac reverse remodeling after the Maze procedure.

J Cardiothorac Surg·2026-10-19·PMID 42894221
房颤与消融 Journal Article

Hee Jeong Lee、YoHan Bae、Jung Uk Woo 等 8 位作者

英文摘要

BACKGROUND: Surgical success after the Maze procedure is conventionally defined as "freedom from atrial fibrillation (AF)," often grouping junctional rhythm (JR) with normal sinus rhythm (NSR). However, the specific hemodynamic impact of JR remains poorly understood. This study investigated whether JR truly represents a successful outcome compared to NSR and AF. METHODS: We retrospectively analyzed patients undergoing the Maze procedure (2012-2024). After designating the first postoperative month as a blanking period for rhythm instability, we included only patients maintaining a consistent rhythm without crossover from 1 to 12 months postoperatively: AF (n = 71), NSR (n = 213), and JR (n = 35). Outcomes included cardiac reverse remodeling-assessed by left atrial contractile strain (LASct) and chamber dimensions-and permanent pacemaker (PPM) implantation. RESULTS: In the IPTW-adjusted cohort, the NSR group achieved significant cardiac reverse remodeling, characterized by improved LVEF (58.5%), reduced LA volume index (75.1 mL/m²), and notable recovery of LASct. In stark contrast, the JR group failed to show any reverse remodeling or mechanical recovery, exhibiting hemodynamic stagnation indistinguishable from the AF group. Consequently, no significant difference in cardiac function was observed between the JR and AF groups. Furthermore, the JR group faced a drastically elevated risk of PPM implantation compared to both the NSR and AF groups (P < 0.001). CONCLUSIONS: JR is not a benign alternative to sinus rhythm; it mimics AF in preventing cardiac reverse remodeling and carries a high risk of pacemaker dependency. Therefore, JR should be clinically differentiated from NSR and recognized as a suboptimal hemodynamic outcome similar to AF.

一句话结论

Maze 术后持续交界性心律并非良性结局：其心脏逆重构与机械恢复情况与 AF 无异，且永久起搏器植入风险显著高于 NSR 与 AF 两组。

研究动机

外科 Maze 手术的成功传统上以“无 AF”定义，因而常把交界性心律（JR）与正常窦性心律（NSR）合并视为成功。但 JR 失去了房室同步与心房收缩功能，其血流动力学后果尚不清楚，既往证据也未明确 JR 是否真能与 NSR 等同，这直接影响术后抗凝、起搏器决策与疗效评价标准。

方法

单中心回顾性队列研究，纳入 2012—2024 年接受 Maze 手术的患者。将术后第 1 个月设为空白期，仅纳入术后 1—12 个月心律稳定、未发生组间交叉者：AF 组 71 例、NSR 组 213 例、JR 组 35 例。终点为心脏逆重构（以左房收缩应变 LASct 及心腔径线评估）与永久起搏器（PPM）植入。采用 IPTT 加权（即逆概率治疗加权）校正混杂后进行组间比较。

结果

在 IPTW 校正队列中，NSR 组出现显著逆重构：LVEF 58.5%，左房容积指数降至 75.1 mL/m²，LASct 明显恢复；JR 组则无任何逆重构或机械功能恢复，血流动力学状态与 AF 组难以区分，JR 与 AF 两组心功能无显著差异。JR 组 PPM 植入风险显著高于 NSR 组和 AF 组（P < 0.001）。摘要未报告各终点的效应量及 95%CI。

结论与意义

JR 不能视为窦性心律的良性替代：它和 AF 一样妨碍心脏逆重构，并带来高起搏依赖风险。临床上应将 JR 与 NSR 区分开，把 JR 归为与 AF 相近的次优血流动力学结局，并据此加强术后心律监测与起搏器评估。局限为单中心回顾性设计、JR 组样本量小（35 例）、随访仅 12 个月，且摘要未报告效应量与置信区间。

Palpitations in children and adolescents in the emergency department: a state-of-the-art narrative review of arrhythmia risk, diagnostic strategy, and disposition.

Cardiol Young·2026-10-19·PMID 42894485
室性心律失常 Journal Article Review

Khaled Abouelmagd、Temitomi Jane Oyedele、Gabriela Berger 等 9 位作者

英文摘要

BACKGROUND: Palpitations are not an uncommon complaint in the paediatric emergency department, causing significant anxiety for families concerned about serious heart disease. However, most cases are benign, often stemming from normal physiological responses like fever or anxiety. Primary cardiac arrhythmias are rare in this clinical setting. PROBLEM: The main challenge is identifying the few children with dangerous arrhythmias among the many benign cases. Fear of missing a life-threatening condition often leads to extensive, costly, and stressful investigations, even in low-risk patients, without significantly improving detection rates. OBJECTIVE: This review aims to create a structured, risk-based framework for evaluating paediatric palpitations in the emergency department. The goal is to help clinicians efficiently distinguish benign from potentially life-threatening causes, thereby optimising resource use and reducing family anxiety. FINDINGS: A thorough clinical history, physical exam, and a standard 12-lead electrocardiogram are the most effective evaluation tools. "Red-flag" features-such as palpitations with fainting (syncope), onset during exercise, or a family history of sudden cardiac death-successfully identify nearly all children with significant arrhythmias. Barring certain risk factors and any abnormalities on a 12 lead electrocardiogram, a dangerous cardiac condition is highly unlikely. RECOMMENDATIONS: A stratified approach is recommended. Low-risk children (no red-flag features, normal electrocardiogram) can be safely discharged with reassurance. High-risk patients require immediate, aggressive evaluation, including cardiology consultation and potential hospital admission. This strategy improves accuracy, is cost-effective, and enhances patient safety.

一句话结论

儿童及青少年急诊心悸绝大多数为良性，依靠病史、体格检查和标准12导联心电图识别晕厥、运动诱发、猝死家族史等警示特征，可分层安全分流。

研究动机

心悸是儿科急诊常见主诉，家长常担忧严重心脏病，而真正的心律失常罕见。临床难点在于从大量良性病例中筛出少数高危患儿；对漏诊致命性心律失常的恐惧常导致低危患者接受过度、昂贵且增加焦虑的检查，而检出率并未显著提高，既往缺乏结构化的急诊评估框架。

方法

本文为叙述性综述（state-of-the-art narrative review），非原始研究，摘要未报告样本量、纳入人群的具体构成、终点定义或统计方法。作者基于文献汇总，提出以病史、体格检查与标准12导联心电图为核心，并结合警示特征（晕厥、运动诱发心悸、猝死家族史）建立急诊儿童心悸的风险分层评估与处置框架。

结果

综述归纳的核心发现为：详尽的临床病史、体格检查与标准12导联心电图是最有效的评估手段；上述警示特征可识别几乎所有存在显著心律失常的儿童。除特定危险因素及12导联心电图异常外，严重心脏疾病的可能性很低。摘要未报告敏感性、特异性、阳性预测值、95%CI 或 P 值等定量数据。

结论与意义

作者推荐分层处置：无警示特征且心电图正常者属低危，可在充分安抚后安全离院；高危患者需立即积极评估，包括心电生理/心内科会诊并考虑住院。该策略有望提高评估准确性、降低成本并改善患者安全。局限性在于本文为叙述性综述，缺乏原始数据与前瞻性验证，风险分层的效能仍需在儿科急诊人群中进一步研究证实。

英文摘要

BACKGROUND AND PURPOSE: Cardiorespiratory ultrasound motion monitoring may enhance the precision of dose delivery for stereotactic arrhythmia radioablation (STAR) of ventricular tachycardia. This treatment planning study aimed to evaluate the feasibility of photon-based STAR planning in the presence of an ultrasound probe. MATERIALS AND METHODS: For sixteen targets in fourteen patients, three treatment plans were retrospectively created and compared: a reference plan without an ultrasound probe and two plans in which the ultrasound probe was spared from the primary treatment beam (apical or parasternal probe positions). Descriptive and statistical analyses were performed for target/organ dose-volume and treatment beam metrics, conformity-indices and maximum probe exposure. RESULTS: The ultrasound probe could be spared from the primary beam in all cases with D0.035 cm³ < 1 Gy (median 0.2 Gy with apical and 0.4 Gy with parasternal placement) with slightly elevated organ dose-volume metrics and no significant differences in target dose-volume metrics. The conformity-index (50 % isodose line) differed significantly for apical and parasternal probe position (median reference plan 4.0 vs. 5.0 and 4.5, respectively; p < 0.01). The parasternal position gave slightly superior results compared to the apical position (e.g., heart minus planning target volume V12.5 Gy 7.6 % [4.7-13.3] vs. 8.2 % [6.4-12.5]; left lung V10 Gy 3.4 % [1.0-4.5] vs. 4.2 % [1.6-6.8]), albeit in all plans dose guidance parameters remained within clinically acceptable limits. CONCLUSIONS: The ultrasound probe could be spared from the primary beam without compromising photon-based STAR plan quality beyond clinically acceptable deviations. The results support further investigation of ultrasound-based STAR motion management.

Unmasking Brugada Syndrome With Oral Propafenone in a Resource-Limited Setting: A Case Report.

Cureus·2026-10-19·PMID 42894783
遗传与基础 Case Reports Journal Article

Luisa Fernanda Jaramillo Astudillo、Guillermo Adrian Navas Moreira、Patricia Delgado Cedeño 等 5 位作者

英文摘要

Brugada syndrome (BrS) is a cardiac channelopathy predisposing patients to malignant ventricular arrhythmias and sudden cardiac death (SCD) in the absence of structural heart disease. The diagnosis requires documentation of a type 1 Brugada electrocardiographic pattern, which can be unmasked using sodium channel-blocking agents in symptomatic patients presenting with non-diagnostic baseline patterns. We report a case of a 24-year-old male patient with a history of unexplained syncope who exhibited a non-diagnostic type 2 Brugada pattern on a baseline electrocardiogram (ECG). Due to the local unavailability of standard intravenous antiarrhythmics, a pharmacological provocation test was conducted using a single 600 mg oral dose of propafenone. Under continuous cardiac monitoring, the patient safely developed a diagnostic type 1 "coved" electrocardiographic pattern starting at the third hour, confirming the diagnosis of BrS. The ECG returned to its baseline pattern eight hours after the initiation of the test, and no cardiovascular complications occurred. This case highlights the clinical utility and safety of oral propafenone as an effective alternative diagnostic challenge for unmasking BrS in resource-limited environments. Furthermore, it emphasizes the necessity of utilizing extended monitoring protocols due to the specific pharmacokinetic profile and delayed onset of action of oral agents.

英文摘要

Adolescents may present with abnormal ECG findings despite having no structural or electrical evidence of cardiomyopathy. The value of extending family screening to this subgroup remains uncertain. To evaluate the impact of family screening in ECG-positive phenotype-negative adolescents, and determine whether a joint clinical-genetic approach improves the diagnostic yield. In this prospective cohort study, adolescents with an abnormal ECG identified through a nationwide screening program underwent comprehensive clinical/genetic evaluation. Phenotype-negative individuals were included. Relatives underwent clinical screening, with cascade genetic testing offered when probands carried a likely/definite pathogenic (LP/P) cardiomyopathy variant. Abnormal screening outcomes included relatives with (i) de novo cardiomyopathy diagnosis, (ii) early cardiomyopathic traits, or (iii) gene-positive/phenotype-negative (G + P -) status. Sixteen adolescents (0.6%) met inclusion criteria, and 3 (18.8%) were G + (MYBPC3, MYH7, ACTC1). One in five relatives (n = 13/71, 18.3%; 95% CI 11.0-28.9%) were referred, most having an abnormal ECG (12.7%). Nine relatives (12.7%; 95% CI 4.9-20.4%) exhibited early cardiomyopathy traits at baseline. One (1.4%) relative was G + /P - . Cardiomyopathy was diagnosed in 3 (4.2%, 95% CI 0.9-11.8%) relatives at baseline (n = 2 HCM, n = 1 DCM). One relative with early cardiomyopathy traits underwent phenoconversion (NDLVC) during follow-up at 77 months, yielding an overall diagnostic rate of 5.6% (95% CI 2.0-13.8%) in 4 unrelated families. A joint clinical-genetic approach in this subgroup may aid early cardiomyopathy detection in selected families, though findings remain exploratory and require validation in larger cohorts.
