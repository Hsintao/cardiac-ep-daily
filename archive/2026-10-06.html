Thrombospondin-1 and Thrombospondin-2 are associated with biatrial fibrotic remodeling in human valvular heart disease.

Adv Med Sci·2026-10-06·PMID 42838307
房颤与消融 Journal Article

Daniel-Dumitru Nișulescu、Ariana Violeta Nicoraș、Cornel Baltă 等 10 位作者

英文摘要

PURPOSE: Atrial fibrosis is a key structural substrate of atrial fibrillation and atrial cardiomyopathy. Thrombospondin-1 (TSP-1) and thrombospondin-2 (TSP-2) regulate TGF-β signaling and matrix organization, but their roles in human atrial remodeling remain incompletely understood. Therefore, this study aimed to compare atrial TSP-1 and TSP-2 expression in mitral and aortic valve disease and examine their associations with fibrosis, inflammation, and adhesion-related remodeling. METHODS: This observational cross-sectional study included 67 cardiac surgery patients: 30 with mitral valve disease, 23 with aortic valve disease, and 14 undergoing coronary artery bypass grafting without significant valvular disease. Left and right atrial tissues were examined using RT-qPCR, ELISA, histology, and immunofluorescence. Molecular assays used pooled tissue preparations. Systemic inflammation was evaluated using the neutrophil-to-lymphocyte ratio. RESULTS: TSP-1 and TSP-2 transcript levels were increased in both valve disease groups, alongside greater collagen deposition and increased inflammatory gene expression. Transcript changes were particularly marked in left atrial tissue from patients with mitral valve disease. TSP-1 protein was significantly increased in right atrial tissue from the mitral valve group, whereas TSP-2 protein did not differ significantly between groups. Cadherin transcript levels were also altered. The neutrophil-to-lymphocyte ratio was higher in both valve disease groups than in the surgical comparator group. CONCLUSIONS: Atrial TSPs expression was associated with fibrotic and inflammatory remodeling in valvular heart disease. Differences between transcript and protein findings and the use of pooled preparations warrant cautious interpretation. These observational findings require validation in independent cohorts and do not establish causality.

结果

两个瓣膜病组的 TSP-1、TSP-2 转录水平均升高，同时胶原沉积增多、炎症基因表达上调；转录改变在二尖瓣病变患者的左房组织中尤为显著。蛋白层面，TSP-1 仅在二尖瓣组右房组织中显著升高，TSP-2 蛋白组间无显著差异；钙黏蛋白（cadherin）转录水平亦发生改变。两个瓣膜病组的 NLR 均高于外科对照组。摘要未报告具体效应量、95%CI 与 P 值。

结论与意义

心房 TSP 表达与瓣膜性心脏病中的纤维化及炎症重构相关，提示 TSP 可能参与人心房基质重构过程。但转录与蛋白结果不一致，且采用混合组织制备，结论需谨慎解读；作为观察性研究不能确立因果关系，尚需独立队列验证。对电生理实践而言，目前尚不构成改变AF基质评估或消融策略的直接依据。

New-Onset Atrial Fibrillation After Alcohol Septal Ablation Versus Surgical Myectomy in Obstructive Hypertrophic Cardiomyopathy: A Propensity-Matched Analysis.

Am J Cardiol·2026-10-06·PMID 42838439
房颤与消融 Journal Article

Mohamad Elgozair、Basel Abdelazeem、Ahmad W Haddad 等 8 位作者

英文摘要

BACKGROUND: Atrial fibrillation (AF) complicates 20-25% of hypertrophic obstructive cardiomyopathy (HOCM) cases and independently predicts heart failure progression, thromboembolism, and mortality. Both surgical myectomy (SM) and alcohol septal ablation (ASA) are associated with new-onset AF through opposing mechanisms; myectomy promoting left atrial reverse remodeling, ASA generating a chronic arrhythmogenic septal scar. Yet no propensity-matched study has directly compared AF incidence between these procedures. OBJECTIVES: To compare the incidence and temporal trajectory of new-onset AF following ASA versus SM at prespecified landmarks of Day 1, Day 7, and Day 30 in a propensity score-matched cohort with 3 years of follow-up. METHODS: Patients with obstructive HOCM who underwent ASA or SM without prior AF were identified in the TriNetX database between Jan. 2010 and Apr. 2023 and were followed for up to 3 years. After 1:1 propensity score matching on demographics, comorbidities, and atrial substrate risk factors, 223 patients remained in each cohort; new-onset AF was ascertained using clinical diagnosis codes applied symmetrically across both groups. RESULTS: Of 1,188 patients (539 ASA, 649 SM), 223 were matched per cohort (mean age 59.5 ± 15.5 years, 37.4% male, 80.3% White); all standardized mean differences were <0.20 after matching. At Day 1, AF occurred in 16.1% vs. 12.1% (ARD 4.0%; HR 1.22, 95% CI 0.74-2.01; p = 0.437). At Day 7, a significant divergence emerged (15.8% vs. 8.4%; ARD 7.4%; RR 1.87, 95% CI 1.10-3.21; p = 0.019). By Day 30, ASA carried a nearly three-fold higher AF risk (15.4% vs. 5.3%; ARD 10.1%; HR 2.75, 95% CI 1.39-5.42; p = 0.039), with AF-free survival of 82.59% vs. 93.83% (log-rank p = 0.002), with progressive Kaplan-Meier separation sustained through 3 years. CONCLUSIONS: In a propensity-matched cohort, ASA was associated with a progressively higher risk of new-onset AF compared with SM, with divergence emerging by Day 7 and widening through Day 30, consistent with substrate reversal after myectomy versus substrate maturation after ASA. These findings support incorporating differential AF risk into procedural selection discussions, extending postprocedural monitoring after ASA, and reassessing early anticoagulation thresholds in ASA patients.

结论与意义

在倾向匹配队列中，ASA 与较 SM 更高的新发房颤风险相关，差异自第7天出现、第30天进一步扩大，符合 SM 后心房基质逆转与 ASA 后基质成熟的不同进程。研究提示应将两种术式的房颤风险差异纳入术式选择讨论，延长 ASA 术后心律监测，并重新评估 ASA 患者早期抗凝的阈值。局限性：回顾性行政数据库研究、依赖诊断编码判定房颤、缺乏两组术后监测强度与房颤负荷（含无症状房颤）数据、可能存在残余混杂，且各时间点发生率的定义在摘要中未明确。

Lyme Carditis Beyond Atrioventricular Block: Cardiac Arrest and Transient Atrial Fibrillation With Preserved Atrioventricular Conduction

Case Rep Cardiol·2026-10-06·PMID 42846156
房颤与消融 Journal Article

Anthony JA、Doktor K

英文摘要

Lyme disease may involve the heart during early dissemination and most commonly produces atrioventricular (AV) conduction abnormalities. PR interval prolongation, therefore, frequently guides clinical suspicion and management. We describe a previously healthy man who presented with seizure-like activity, aborted cardiac arrest, transient atrial fibrillation, and cardiac magnetic resonance findings consistent with acute myocarditis, although AV conduction remained normal. Serologic testing subsequently confirmed Lyme carditis. The patient recovered completely with oral doxycycline. This case illustrates that electrical instability may occur despite preserved PR intervals and reinforces the need for suspicion in patients with compatible systemic features.

Racial and Ethnic Disparities in the Utilization and Outcomes of Catheter Ablation for Atrial Fibrillation and Flutter: a State-of-the-Art Review.

Curr Cardiol Rep·2026-10-06·PMID 42837029
房颤与消融 Journal Article Review

Daniel Antwi-Amoabeng、T David Gbadebo

英文摘要

PURPOSE OF REVIEW: Catheter ablation (CA) has become a first-line option for rhythm control strategy for atrial fibrillation (AF) and atrial flutter (AFL). We synthesize recent evidence regarding racial and ethnic disparities in CA utilization in the United States and examine the structural mechanism driving them. RECENT FINDINGS: Black patients are 28 to 36% less likely to undergo CA for AF or AFL compared to White patients, with similarly reduced odds (22 - 35%) observed among Hispanic and Asian patients for AF. While subgroup analysis from clinical trials like CABANA demonstrate survival benefits of CA among minority cohorts, real-world data reveal that the very populations that stand to benefit the most are the least likely to receive CA. Public insurance models, upstream referral bottlenecks, geographic specialty deserts, and clinician-level implicit bias serve as primary structural drivers of these disparities. Racial and ethnic gaps persist in CA utilization and outcomes for both AF and AFL. AF/AFL prevalence is influenced by racial and ancestral biology while CA access disparity is driven by race as a social construct. These two phenomena require fundamentally different investigative frameworks and intervention targets.

The Left Atrial Appendage: Anatomy, Electrophysiology, and Clinical Implications for Atrial Fibrillation and Stroke.

Curr Cardiol Rep·2026-10-06·PMID 42837014
房颤与消融 Journal Article Review

Adriana Daniela Sârb、Csilla-Andrea Eötvös、Teodora Avram 等 15 位作者

英文摘要

PURPOSE OF REVIEW: The left atrial appendage (LAA) is nowadays recognized as both an arrhythmogenic substrate and the dominant source of thromboembolism in atrial fibrillation (AF). In this review we tried to summarize recent data on the anatomy, physiology, and imaging of the LAA, and examine how these findings help guide rhythm-control and stroke-prevention strategies. RECENT FINDINGS: High-resolution mapping confirms the LAA as a non-pulmonary-vein trigger source in persistent and post-ablation AF, supporting LAA electrical isolation in selected patients, though isolation may induce mechanical standstill and mandate continued thromboprophylaxis. The 2024 ESC guidelines reframed stroke-risk assessment around the sex-neutral CHA₂DS₂-VA score within the AF-CARE pathway. An important recent study was the OPTION trial, which showed that LAA closure after AF ablation was as effective as oral anticoagulation for preventing death, stroke, or systemic embolism and was associated with less non-procedure-related bleeding. These findings support the use of LAA closure in selected patients after ablation. New imaging and emerging computational tools may also improve patient selection and help guide device sizing. A comprehensive assessment of the anatomy, function, and electrical activity of the LAA can help adapt AF treatment to each patient.

英文摘要

BACKGROUND AND AIMS: Randomized evidence comparing conduction system pacing (CSP) and biventricular pacing (BVP) following atrioventricular node ablation in patients with refractory atrial fibrillation (AF) and heart failure (HF) remains limited. This study evaluated whether CSP with left bundle branch area pacing is non-inferior to BVP in patients undergoing atrioventricular node ablation. METHODS: CONDUCT-AF was an investigator-initiated, multicentre, randomized trial conducted across 10 European centres. Patients with refractory AF, HF with left ventricular ejection fraction (LVEF) < 50%, narrow QRS duration (≤120 ms), and N-terminal pro-B-type natriuretic peptide > 600 ng/L were randomized 1:1 to CSP or BVP. The primary endpoint was the change in LVEF after 6 months, assessed by an independent core laboratory. Secondary endpoints included echocardiographic, clinical, and procedural outcomes. Analyses followed the intention-to-treat principle. RESULTS: Eighty-two patients were randomized (mean age 72 ± 7 years, 46% male). At 6 months, LVEF improved similarly in CSP {from 36% [95% confidence interval (CI) 33-39] to 46% [95% CI 44-49]; P < .001} and BVP group [from 34% (95% CI 31-36) to 46% (95% CI 43-49); P < .001]. The between-group difference was .4% (95% CI -3.1-3.8; P = .002 for non-inferiority). Changes in left ventricular volumes, clinical parameters, and composite endpoint of worsening HF or cardiovascular death were comparable between groups. Paced QRS [mean difference -14 ms (95% CI -19 to -9; P < .001)] and procedural and fluoroscopy times were significantly shorter with CSP compared with BVP. CONCLUSIONS: In HF patients with refractory AF undergoing atrioventricular node ablation, CSP with left bundle branch area pacing was non-inferior to BVP for LVEF improvement at 6 months, providing comparable clinical outcomes and a favourable procedural profile.
