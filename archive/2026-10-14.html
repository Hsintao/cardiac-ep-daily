Pulsed field ablation for persistent atrial fibrillation with atrioventricular reentrant tachycardia under tubeless general anesthesia: a case report.

AME Case Rep·2026-10-14·PMID 42871365
房颤与消融 Case Reports Journal Article

Weida Qiu、Xintong Wang、Shenrong Liu 等 5 位作者

英文摘要

BACKGROUND: Radiofrequency ablation (RFA) combined with left atrial appendage closure (LAAC) has long been used in the treatment of atrial fibrillation (AF). Pulsed field ablation (PFA) is now increasingly adopted due to its great precision and improved safety profile. This report presents a rare case of PFA performed for persistent AF and atrioventricular reentrant tachycardia (AVRT), along with LAAC, under non-intubated general anesthesia. CASE DESCRIPTION: A 36-year-old male patient presented with a 10-year history of recurrent chest tightness and palpitations, which had worsened during the week prior to admission. He was diagnosed with dilated cardiomyopathy and AF 4 months ago and had a previous cerebral infarction, with a CHA2DS2-VASc score of 3 and a HAS-BLED score of 1. A one-stop procedure combining PFA and LAAC was planned. During ablation at the roof of the right superior pulmonary vein, AF converted to sinus rhythm and subsequently developed spontaneous tachycardia, suspected to be AVRT due to a left-sided accessory pathway near CS1-2. Therefore, ablation of the accessory pathway and mitral isthmus was performed, and atrioventricular separation and mitral isthmus block were confirmed. A total of 78 discharges were delivered throughout the procedure, and repeated high-frequency atrial stimulation did not induce atrial flutter or AF. LAAC was then completed as planned. Electrocardiogram (ECG) showed sinus rhythm, and echocardiography demonstrated improved cardiac remodeling at 3-month follow-up. CONCLUSIONS: AVRT is a type of supraventricular tachycardia caused by the presence of an accessory pathway due to congenital structural abnormalities. Our case reports an AVRT occurrence during PFA for AF and provides significant evidence that PFA is effective and safe for accessory pathway ablation during AF ablation.

方法

单例病例报告。患者为36岁男性，反复胸闷心悸10年、入院前1周加重；4个月前诊断扩张型心肌病合并AF，既往脑梗死，CHA2DS2-VASc评分3分、HAS-BLED评分1分。计划行PFA联合LAAC的一站式手术，在无气管插管全麻下进行。术中于右上肺静脉顶部消融时AF转复窦律并出现自发性心动过速，疑为CS1-2附近左侧旁路介导的AVRT，遂加做旁路消融及二尖瓣峡部消融，以房室分离和二尖瓣峡部阻滞作为消融成功终点；术后以高频心房刺激验证不能诱发房扑/房颤，随后按计划完成LAAC。随访评估包括心电图与超声心动图。摘要未报告具体统计方法（为单例描述性报告）。

结果

术中AF在右上肺静脉顶部消融时转为窦律，随后出现自发性心动过速，判断为CS1-2附近左侧旁路所致的AVRT；行旁路及二尖瓣峡部消融后确认房室分离与二尖瓣峡部阻滞。全程共放电78次，反复高频心房刺激未诱发出心房扑动或心房颤动，随后按计划完成左心耳封堵。术后心电图示窦性心律；3个月随访超声心动图示心脏重构改善。围术期并发症、封堵器残余漏及具体超声参数摘要未报告。

结论与意义

本病例提示，在持续性房颤行PFA过程中可新发或暴露旁路相关AVRT，且PFA可用于同期旁路消融，并可与LAAC在同一非插管全麻手术中完成，术中电生理终点（房室分离、二尖瓣峡部阻滞）及高频刺激验证均达标，短期随访窦律维持、心脏重构改善。该结果支持PFA在房颤合并旁路患者中的有效性与安全性，但因系单例报告、随访仅3个月、缺乏对照与长期心律失常复发及封堵器结局数据，结论需更大样本与更长随访验证，不能据此改变现有实践标准。

A predictive model for heart failure in patients with hypertension and persistent atrial fibrillation: A retrospective single-center study.

Chin Med Sci J·2026-10-14·PMID 42875145
房颤与消融 Journal Article

Qun-Yuan Wang、Cui-Ping Pan、Yu-Ting Zou 等 9 位作者

英文摘要

OBJECTIVE: This study aimed to develop and validate a predictive nomogram for heart failure in patients with hypertension and persistent atrial fibrillation (HT-PeAF). METHODS: We retrospectively analyzed clinical data from patients with HT-PeAF hospitalized at Chinese People's Liberation Army General Hospital between 2010 and 2019. Predictive variables were selected based on multivariable logistic regression analysis and clinical relevance, which were then incorporated into a clinical nomogram. Model performance was evaluated using the area under the receiver operating characteristic curve (AUC) for discrimination, the Hosmer-Lemeshow test for calibration, and decision curve analysis for clinical utility. RESULTS: Of the 395 enrolled patients, 151 experienced heart failure. Nine predictive variables were identified: prior catheter ablation, low-density lipoprotein cholesterol, hemoglobin, uric acid, creatinine, total bilirubin, fasting blood glucose, red blood cell distribution width, and coronary artery disease. The nomogram demonstrated good discriminative ability (AUC = 0.77, 95% CI: 0.72-0.81), good calibration (P = 0.44), and favorable clinical utility. CONCLUSION: This accessible tool facilitates individualized risk stratification for heart failure in HT-PeAF patients, though prospective validation in multicenter Asian cohorts is warranted.

Association of Iron Homeostasis With One-Year All-Cause Mortality Among Intensive Care Unit Patients with Atrial Fibrillation: A Retrospective Study.

Clin Cardiol·2026-10-14·PMID 42872906
房颤与消融 Journal Article

Mingze Wu、Juan Hua、Yao Zhou 等 7 位作者

英文摘要

BACKGROUND: Perturbations in iron homeostasis can substantially affect cardiovascular outcomes. This study aims to evaluate the prognostic value of various iron homeostasis indicators for all-cause mortality in critically ill AF patients. METHOD: Data for this retrospective study were obtained from the Medical Information Mart for Intensive Care IV database. Based on the iron homeostasis-related indicators (ferritin, serum iron, transferrin, and total iron-binding capacity [TIBC]), the risks of 1-year mortality among critically ill patients with atrial fibrillation were calculated using Cox proportional-hazards regression. Associations of iron homeostasis indicators with all-cause mortality in critically ill AF patients were assessed via Kaplan-Meier curves, restricted cubic splines, and subgroup analyses. RESULTS: After multivariable adjustment, patients in the highest ferritin quartile had a greater risk of 1-year mortality than those in the lowest quartile (Q4 HR 1.277, 95% CI 1.083-1.506, p < 0.01). In contrast, the highest quartiles of transferrin (Q4 HR 0.732, 95% CI 0.622-0.861, p < 0.01) and TIBC (Q4 HR 0.732, 95% CI 0.622-0.861, p < 0.01) were associated with lower mortality. No significant association was observed between serum iron quartiles and 1-year mortality (Q4 HR 1.067, 95% CI 0.914-1.245, p = 0.41). Restricted cubic spline analyses further characterized the exposure and outcome patterns for these iron-related parameters. CONCLUSION: Among critically ill patients with AF, ferritin, transferrin and TIBC levels were independently associated with 1-year all-cause mortality. No significant association was observed for serum iron.

Association Between Left Atrial Volume Index (LAVI) and Glycemic Control (HbA1c) in Patients With Atrial Fibrillation and Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus.

Cureus·2026-10-14·PMID 42871596
房颤与消融 Journal Article

Marvelous O Popoola、Muhammad Abbas Shahid、Faiq Aftab 等 10 位作者

英文摘要

BACKGROUND: Glycemic control and left atrial remodeling in patients with concomitant type 2 diabetes mellitus (T2DM) and atrial fibrillation (AF) remain unclear. In this study, we examined the association between glycated hemoglobin (HbA1c) and left atrial volume index (LAVI) in this high-risk population. METHODS: A total of 700 patients with AF and T2DM were included in this retrospective study. We used medical records to obtain HbA1c and echocardiographic parameters. We assessed LAVI as a continuous variable and as a binary variable (enlarged LAVI > 34 mL/m²). We used Spearman correlation, multivariable linear regression, and binary logistic regression to examine the relationship between HbA1c and LAVI. RESULTS: There was a significant association between higher HbA1c and greater LAVI (Spearman's ρ = 0.287, p < 0.001). After multivariable adjustment, each 1% increase in HbA1c was associated with a 1.678 mL/m² increase in LAVI. Higher HbA1c was independently associated with increased odds of enlarged LAVI (adjusted odds ratio (OR) = 1.701, 95% confidence interval (CI): 1.391-2.081, p < 0.001). Hypertension, heart failure, and chronic kidney disease were also independently associated with enlarged LAVI. CONCLUSION: In patients with concurrent AF and T2DM, higher HbA1c was independently associated with larger LAVI and higher odds of left atrial enlargement (LAE). Future prospective studies are warranted to determine whether improved glycemic control can influence atrial remodeling.

Oral Obesity Medicines in the Era of Cardiovascular-Kidney-Metabolic Syndrome (CKM): Beyond Weight Loss Toward Disease Modification.

Drug Des Devel Ther·2026-10-14·PMID 42871841
房颤与消融 Journal Article Review

Abdulhameed Alhazmi、Carel W Le Roux

英文摘要

Obesity is a major driver of Cardiovascular-Kidney-Metabolic (CKM) syndrome and contributes to the development of atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease, heart failure, atrial fibrillation, chronic kidney disease, and type 2 diabetes. Historically, oral obesity pharmacotherapy was evaluated primarily according to weight loss efficacy. However, the emergence of the CKM framework has shifted therapeutic priorities toward modifying obesity related complications and improving long term clinical outcomes. This review summarizes currently approved oral obesity medications, including first generation agents (orlistat, phentermine/topiramate, and naltrexone/bupropion) and second generation incretin-based therapies (oral semaglutide and orforglipron), within the context of CKM syndrome. First generation agents provide modest-to-moderate weight loss and variable metabolic benefits, but evidence supporting cardiovascular or kidney outcome improvement remains limited and largely observational. In contrast, oral incretin-based therapies demonstrate broader effects across the CKM spectrum, including substantial weight reduction, improved glycemic control, favorable effects on blood pressure and triglycerides, and emerging kidney benefits. Among currently available oral agents, oral semaglutide has the strongest evidence, with the SOUL trial demonstrating a reduction in major adverse cardiovascular events in high-risk individuals with type 2 diabetes. Signals suggesting benefits in heart failure and atrial fibrillation are encouraging but remain exploratory. Orforglipron represents the first non-peptide oral glucagon-like peptide-1 receptor agonist and offers simplified administration with promising metabolic efficacy; however, cardiovascular, kidney, heart failure, and arrhythmia outcome data are still awaited. Collectively, the role of oral obesity pharmacotherapy is evolving from weight management alone toward comprehensive CKM care.
