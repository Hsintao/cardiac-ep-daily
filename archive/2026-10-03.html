Typical atrial flutter was terminated and sinus rhythm was restored. We discussed the mechanism of changes in the coronary sinus atrial electrograms.

一句话结论

本文报告一例典型心房扑动经三尖瓣峡部消融终止并恢复窦性心律后，冠状静脉窦心房电图发生变化，并探讨其机制。

研究动机

典型心房扑动的根治性治疗是峡部依赖折返环的三尖瓣峡部消融；消融后冠状静脉窦（CS）心房电图形态改变究竟反映传导路径的重新分配、消融本身造成的局部电位改变，还是仍存在未被察觉的缓慢传导，这一问题在临床上尚未充分阐明，而识别这类改变有助于判断消融是否达到双向阻滞以及是否可能出现复发。为此，作者通过本病例分析了消融后 CS 心房电图改变的机制。

方法

研究类型为病例报告，摘要未报告患者年龄、性别及合并症等基线信息，也未报告样本量的具体构成。干预为针对典型心房扑动实施三尖瓣峡部（cavotricuspid isthmus）消融。观察指标为消融前后冠状静脉窦心房电图的形态与激动顺序变化。摘要未报告终点定义、随访方案及统计方法，因此本研究不适用组间比较分析。

结果

摘要仅报告典型心房扑动被终止并恢复窦性心律，消融后冠状静脉窦心房电图出现改变，并据此对机制进行了讨论。摘要未报告扑动周期长度、消融能量与放电次数、峡部双向阻滞的验证结果、CS 电图的具体形态与激动时序参数，以及任何效应量、95% 置信区间或 P 值。

结论与意义

该病例提示：三尖瓣峡部消融后冠状静脉窦心房电图的改变应结合激动顺序与阻滞验证结果综合判读，避免将消融造成的局部电位变化误判为传导恢复或残余缓慢传导。但本文为单例报告，没有对照与量化数据，结论不能外推，仅能作为提出机制假说的线索，有待更大样本的电生理标测研究加以验证。

[Atrial fibrillation screening-reading the P-wave].

Inn Med (Heidelb)·2026-10-02·PMID 42827193
房颤与消融 English Abstract Journal Article Review

Felix Wiedmann、Frederike Marie Weitkamp、Constanze Schmidt

英文摘要

BACKGROUND: Atrial fibrillation (AF) is frequently intermittent and may therefore remain undetected on a short resting electrocardiogram (ECG). At the same time, a sinus-rhythm ECG contains information on the underlying atrial substrate. OBJECTIVE: To review conventional and artificial intelligence (AI)-based ECG markers of an atrial risk phenotype and their potential role in targeted AF screening. MATERIALS AND METHODS: Narrative appraisal of current consensus and guideline documents and of clinical, electrophysiological, and population-based studies on P‑wave parameters, AI-ECG, and risk-adapted AF screening. RESULTS: P‑wave duration, interatrial block, P‑wave terminal force in V1, and other parameters are associated with atrial remodelling and future AF. AI models additionally extract high-dimensional patterns from sinus-rhythm ECGs and can predict occult or future AF. Initial prospective data indicate that such risk signals can enrich the diagnostic yield of prolonged rhythm monitoring. CONCLUSION: The most plausible current clinical application is risk-adapted selection for prolonged rhythm monitoring. A P-wave or AI-based risk score neither establishes a diagnosis of AF nor, by itself, constitutes an indication for treatment.

一句话结论

综述指出，窦律心电图的P波参数与AI-ECG模型可识别心房基质风险表型，用于富集高危人群以筛选隐匿性AF，但其本身不能诊断AF或作为治疗指征。

AF常为阵发性，短时静息心电图易漏诊；而窦律心电图本身携带心房基质信息。既往指南对如何用P波及AI指标指导靶向AF筛查缺乏系统梳理，本文旨在评估这些心房风险表型标志物在风险适应性筛查中的潜在作用。

结果

P波时限、房间传导阻滞、V1导联P波终末电势等参数与心房重构及未来AF发生相关；AI模型可自窦律心电图提取高维模式并预测隐匿性或未来AF。初步前瞻性数据提示，此类风险信号可提高延长节律监测的诊断阳性率。摘要未报告具体效应量、95%CI或P值。

结论与意义

目前最合理的临床应用是以P波或AI风险评分进行风险分层，据此选择患者接受延长节律监测；该类评分既不确立AF诊断，其本身也不构成治疗指征。对电生理实践而言，其价值在于优化筛查资源配置与监测对象选择；局限性在于本文为叙述性综述，缺乏定量合并证据，前瞻性验证仍有限。

The Coronary Sinus: A clinical perspective for the electrophysiologist

Indian Pacing Electrophysiol J·2026-10-03·PMID 42829189
室性心律失常 Review Journal Article

Amin A、Makkar N、Gupta I 等 6 位作者

英文摘要

The coronary sinus (CS) is a specialized cardiac structure whose embryologic and anatomic features may contribute to arrhythmogenesis. Composed of both venous and myocardial elements, it can be conceptualized as a distinct cardiac chamber with connections to both the left and right atria-characteristics that make it an important site for the initiation and maintenance of atrial arrhythmias, as well as a key structure in explaining the underlying physiology of atrioventricular nodal reentrant tachycardia (AVNRT) and the origin of epicardial bypass tracts. The vein, ligament, and myocardial bundle of Marshall arise from the proximal CS and maintain electrical connections with the left atrium and the left-sided pulmonary veins. Because of their electrophysiological and autonomic properties, these structures may contribute to both atrial and ventricular arrhythmias. Furthermore, the proximity of CS tributaries to the ventricular myocardium provides access for the mapping and ablation of selected ventricular arrhythmias, complementing the established role of these vessels in left ventricular pacing.

一句话结论

综述指出，冠状窦由静脉与心肌成分共同构成，可视为一个连接左右心房的独立心腔，既是房性心律失常发生和维持的关键部位，也是解释AVNRT、心外膜旁路及Marshall结构相关心律失常的重要解剖基础，并为部分室性心律失常提供标测与消融通路。

研究动机

冠状窦的胚胎学与解剖学特征提示其可能参与致心律失常过程，但临床上常仅将其视为解剖标志或左室起搏导线入路。既往证据的缺口在于：未充分把冠状窦当作一个具有静脉与心肌双重成分、与左右心房均有连接的独立心腔来理解；对Marshall静脉、韧带及心肌束与左房、左侧肺静脉之间的电连接及其自主神经特性，在房性与室性心律失常发生机制中的作用认识不足；对冠状窦分支毗邻心室肌这一特点，在室性心律失常标测与消融中的潜在价值也缺乏系统梳理。

结果

摘要未报告任何定量数据，因此没有效应量、95% 置信区间或 P 值可供引用。定性要点包括：冠状窦兼具静脉与心肌成分，可被概念化为一个独立心腔，并与左右心房相连；这些特征使其成为房性心律失常发生和维持的重要部位，也是解释 AVNRT 生理机制及心外膜旁路起源的关键结构；近端冠状窦发出 Marshall 静脉、韧带与心肌束，并保持与左房及左侧肺静脉的电连接，由于其电生理与自主神经特性，可能参与房性与室性心律失常；冠状窦分支邻近心室肌，为特定室性心律失常的标测与消融提供入路，补充了这些血管在左室起搏中的既有作用。

结论与意义

综述强调，对电生理医生而言，冠状窦不只是一个解剖标志或起搏入路，而是一个需要系统评估的致心律失常结构：理解其心肌袖、Marshall 结构与左房及左侧肺静脉的电连接，有助于阐明 AVNRT、心外膜旁路以及起源于 Marshall 结构的房性心律失常的机制，并优化消融策略；冠状窦分支与心室肌的毗邻关系也为部分室性心律失常提供标测与消融通路。局限性在于：本文为叙述性综述，摘要未报告系统检索策略、纳入文献数量及任何定量分析，所述机制与临床推论缺乏原始数据支持，其中许多尚待前瞻性研究验证。

Effect of a structured nurse-led empowerment protocol on knowledge, practical skills, and self-care behaviours among patients with permanent pacemakers: a quasi-experimental study

BMC Nurs·2026-10-03·PMID 42829445
器械治疗 research-article Journal Article 开放获取

Kamel WNS、Assaf ARA、Shehata AM 等 4 位作者

英文摘要

Background Patients with permanent pacemakers frequently demonstrate insufficient knowledge and inadequate self-care behaviours, which increase the risk of avoidable post-implantation complications. Structured, theory-informed empowerment protocols may improve these outcomes, yet robust evidence from low- and middle-income countries remains limited. Grounded in Bandura's self-efficacy theory and a patient-empowerment model, this study evaluated the effect of a nurse-led empowerment protocol on knowledge, practical skills, and self-care behaviours among patients with permanent pacemakers.Methods A non-equivalent control-group, pretest-posttest quasi-experimental design was used and reported in accordance with the Transparent Reporting of Evaluations with Nonrandomized Designs (TREND) statement. Seventy adults with newly implanted permanent pacemakers were recruited from Tanta University Hospitals, Egypt, between December 2025 and April 2026. Because the two arms were recruited in consecutive calendar periods to prevent educational contamination, allocation was non-random and group membership is confounded with time period. The control group (n = 35) received routine hospital care; the study group (n = 35) additionally received a structured empowerment protocol (four individual sessions of 30-45 min each, using illustrated Arabic booklets, demonstration and return demonstration, followed by weekly telephone reinforcement during the first month). Three co-primary outcomes were assessed at baseline, immediately post-intervention, and at one-month follow-up: knowledge (Pacemaker-Related Knowledge Questionnaire, 0-20), practice competence (Observational Checklist, 0-50), and self-care behaviour (Self-Care Behaviours Scale, 0-20). Data were analysed using repeated-measures ANOVA (group × time) with Greenhouse-Geisser correction and independent and paired t-tests; a family-wise threshold of α = 0.017 (0.05/3) was applied across the three co-primary group × time tests. Between-group effect sizes and 95% confidence intervals (CIs) were computed.Results The study group improved significantly more than the control group on all three co-primary outcomes. Mean knowledge scores rose from 6.51 ± 2.45 to 15.31 ± 3.46 post-intervention (control: 5.57 ± 2.47 to 7.46 ± 5.60; group × time F(1.43, 97.38) = 148.09, p < 0.001, η²p = 0.685; between-group post-intervention difference 7.85 points, 95% CI 5.67-10.03, Cohen's d = 1.69). Practice competence improved from 16.29 ± 2.87 to 41.17 ± 10.00 in the study group (control: 14.91 ± 3.33 to 18.54 ± 12.21; F(1.59, 108.12) = 120.34, p < 0.001, η²p = 0.639; difference 22.63, 95% CI 17.40-27.86, d = 2.03). Self-care behaviour scores increased from 13.63 ± 3.11 to 19.00 ± 3.23 in the study group (control: 12.86 ± 2.30 to 15.00 ± 3.10; F(2, 136) = 49.85, p < 0.001, η²p = 0.423; difference 4.00, 95% CI 2.52-5.48, d = 1.26). All three interactions remained significant at the family-wise threshold, and the groups did not differ significantly on any recorded baseline characteristic. Gains were partially sustained at one-month follow-up.Conclusions In this non-randomised study, participants who received a structured, nurse-led empowerment protocol showed substantial and partially sustained gains in knowledge, practical skills, and self-care behaviours, whereas the corresponding gains observed under routine hospital care alone were minimal. Because the two groups were recruited in consecutive calendar periods rather than randomised concurrently, and because outcome assessment was unblinded, these results are best interpreted as an association observed under a quasi-experimental design rather than as causal evidence equivalent to that of a randomised controlled trial. They nonetheless provide a reasonable basis for evaluating systematic, interactive empowerment programmes-incorporating individualised education and scheduled reinforcement-as an addition to routine post-implantation nursing care, and for testing them in randomised, attention-controlled trials with blinded assessment and longer follow-up.Trial registration ClinicalTrials.gov, NCT07743346. Registered on 26 July 2026. Retrospectively registered. Available at: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07743346 .

结论与意义

在这项非随机类实验研究中，接受护士主导结构化赋能方案的患者在知识、操作技能和自我照护行为方面获得大幅且部分持续的改善，而单纯常规院内护理的改善有限。由于两组按连续日历时间段而非同期随机分组、且结局评估未设盲，结果宜解释为类实验设计下观察到的关联，而非等同于RCT的因果证据；该方案为在随机、注意对照、盲法评估及更长随访的试验中进一步验证系统化互动式赋能教育（个体化教育加计划性强化）作为起搏器植入术后常规护理补充的价值提供了依据。研究为单中心、样本量小、回顾性注册（NCT07743346），外推需谨慎。

Risk Stratification for Pericardial Effusion After Leadless Pacemaker Implantation　- Multicenter Study

Circ J·2026-10-03·PMID 42830528
器械治疗 Journal Article

Komai Y、Minamiguchi H、Ishimura M 等 22 位作者

英文摘要

Background Postoperative pericardial effusion (PPE) is a known complication associated with the use of leadless pacemakers (LPMs). The risk score (RS) is a useful tool for risk stratification. This study evaluated the utility of risk stratification using the RS in Japanese patients who received the Micra LPM.Methods and results We retrospectively enrolled 443 patients who received Micra LPMs that were implanted at 4 institutions between August 2017 and August 2024. Patients were risk stratified using the RS into 3 groups: low risk (score ≤0), intermediate risk (score 1), and high risk (score ≥2). PPEs were observed in 9.0% (20/221), 11.7% (9/77) and 17.9% (26/145) of patients in the low-, intermediate-, and high-risk groups, respectively (P=0.041). In multivariable analysis, chronic obstructive pulmonary disease was a significant risk factor (P=0.014) for PPE, whereas prior cardiac surgery significantly reduced the risk of PPE (P=0.023). Older age, being female, and hemodialysis were not identified as risk factors. Subsequently, we modified the RS (mRS). Using the mRS, PPEs were observed in 8.8% (27/306), 16.5% (14/85), and 26.9% (14/52) of patients in the low-, intermediate-, and high-risk groups, respectively (P<0.001).Conclusions The original RS is useful for PPE risk stratification after Micra LPM implantation. However, the mRS provides better risk discrimination and may therefore be a more suitable tool for use in the Japanese population.

研究动机

无导线起搏器（LPM）植入后心包积液（PPE）是已知并发症，一旦发生可致心脏压塞，需提前识别高危人群。既往已有针对Micra植入的PPE风险评分（RS），但其在日本人群中的适用性及分层效能尚缺乏验证，且原始评分纳入的部分危险因素（如高龄、女性、血液透析）在不同人群中权重可能不同，因此有必要在日本人群中重新评估并优化风险分层工具。

方法

回顾性多中心研究，纳入2017年8月至2024年8月间4家机构植入Micra LPM的443例患者。按原始RS分为低危（评分≤0，n=221）、中危（评分1，n=77）、高危（评分≥2，n=145）三层，比较各组PPE发生率；采用多因素分析筛选PPE的危险因素，并据此构建改良风险评分（mRS）重新分层，评价其区分能力。PPE为主要观察终点。摘要未报告mRS具体构成条目及判别力（如C统计量）等细节。
