Heart failure and atrial fibrillation prediction from non-gated chest CT scans using AI-based cardiac chamber volumetry: an AI-CVD study within the multi-ethnic study of atherosclerosis (MESA).

Am J Prev Cardiol·2026-10-18·PMID 42893186
房颤与消融 Journal Article

Seyed Reza Mirjalili、Kyle Atlas、Anthony P Reeves 等 32 位作者

英文摘要

BACKGROUND: We previously demonstrated that AI-derived cardiac chamber volumetry in ECG-gated CT scans predicts atrial fibrillation (AF) and heart failure (HF). Since most chest CTs are non-gated, and the American Heart Association recently highlighted their value for opportunistic cardiovascular risk assessment using AI-based coronary artery calcium scoring, we extended this concept to evaluate whether AI-derived chamber volumetry from non-gated CTs can reliably predict HF and AF.s. METHODS: We compared AI-derived chamber volumetry from non-gated and ECG-gated chest CTs in 2053 Multi-Ethnic Study of Atherosclerosis (MESA) Exam 5 participants using mean and standard-deviation of absolute differences, intraclass correlation coefficients (ICC), and Cohen's κ. Variability between paired ECG-gated scans was determined by analyzing scans acquired 2 min apart in 5749 MESA Exam 1 participants. Predictive performance for incident HF and AF was compared using cumulative incidence curves, age- and sex-adjusted C-indices, and non-inferiority testing (δ=0.05). RESULTS: Non-gated scans demonstrated excellent overall agreement with ECG-gated scans, with comparable performance for detecting cardiac chamber enlargement (98.8-99.2%, κ=0.76-0.84), relative to agreement observed between paired ECG-gated scans (98.6-98.9%, κ=0.72-0.77). Cumulative incidence curves for HF and AF stratified by quartiles showed substantial overlap. Age- and sex-adjusted C-indices were comparable for left atrial volume (0.75 vs 0.74, p = 0.001, non-inferiority p < 0.001), ventricular volume (both 0.77, p = 0.97, non-inferiority p < 0.001), and ventricular ratio (both 0.74, p = 0.57, non-inferiority p < 0.001) in predicting AF and HF. CONCLUSION: AI-based evaluation of non-gated chest CT may provide reliable cardiac chamber volumetry and cardiovascular outcome prediction, enabling opportunistic screening for HF and AF risk.

研究动机

研究团队此前已证实，心电门控 CT 的 AI 心腔容积测量可预测房颤（AF）与心衰（HF），但临床上绝大多数胸部 CT 为非门控采集，既往证据能否外推存疑。美国心脏协会（AHA）近期强调，非门控 CT 结合 AI 冠脉钙化评分可用于机会性心血管风险评估，因此有必要验证非门控 CT 的心腔容积测量是否同样可靠、是否具备结局预测价值。

方法

基于多族裔动脉粥样硬化研究（MESA）的一致性分析与预后分析。一致性分析纳入 Exam 5 的 2053 名受试者，比较非门控与心电门控胸部 CT 的 AI 心腔容积测量，采用绝对差值的均值与标准差、组内相关系数（ICC）及 Cohen's κ 评价；并以 Exam 1 中 5749 名受试者间隔 2 分钟重复采集的配对心电门控扫描作为扫描间变异度的参照。预后终点为新发 HF 与 AF，统计方法包括累积发病率曲线、年龄与性别校正 C 指数以及非劣效检验（δ=0.05）。

结果

非门控扫描与心电门控扫描总体一致性优秀，检出心腔扩大的符合率为 98.8%–99.2%（κ=0.76–0.84），与配对门控扫描之间的一致性（98.6%–98.9%，κ=0.72–0.77）相当。按心腔容积四分位分层的 HF 与 AF 累积发病率曲线明显重叠。年龄与性别校正 C 指数显示，左房容积为 0.75 vs 0.74（p=0.001，非劣效 p<0.001），心室容积两者均为 0.77（p=0.97，非劣效 p<0.001），心室比值两者均为 0.74（p=0.57，非劣效 p<0.001）；摘要未逐一区分上述数值对应 AF 还是 HF。具体 ICC 与绝对差值数值摘要未报告。

结论与意义

AI 辅助的非门控胸部 CT 心腔容积测量结果可靠，对 HF 与 AF 的预测能力不劣于心电门控 CT，支持在常规胸部 CT 中开展机会性 HF/AF 风险筛查。局限在于该研究为多族裔观察性队列内的分析，缺乏外部验证与前瞻性数据，且从风险识别到临床干预的路径（如何报告、如何随访与治疗）尚未确定，距改变电生理实践仍有距离。

GLP-1 receptor agonist therapy and incident atrial fibrillation in non-diabetic adults with obesity and chronic kidney disease: a propensity matched cohort study.

Int J Cardiol·2026-10-18·PMID 42892471
房颤与消融 Journal Article

Abdulraheem Hassan、Efeturi Okorigba、Chilaka Grace 等 8 位作者

英文摘要

BACKGROUND: Obesity and chronic kidney disease (CKD) are potent risk factors for atrial fibrillation (AF), yet whether GLP-1 receptor agonists (GLP-1RAs) prevent incident AF in non-diabetic adults with obesity and CKD is unexamined. We assessed whether GLP-1RA therapy reduces 1-year AF incidence in this cardiorenal phenotype. METHODS: Using the TriNetX US Collaborative Network (66 healthcare organizations), we conducted a retrospective target trial emulation of non-diabetic adults aged 18 or older with obesity and CKD stages 3-5 diagnosed 2021-2024. Patients receiving semaglutide, liraglutide, or tirzepatide (n = 10,985) were compared with unexposed patients (n = 94,527). After propensity score matching, 7326 patients remained per cohort. The primary outcome was incident AF at 1 year; secondary outcomes included AF subtypes, ventricular tachyarrhythmias, and all-cause hospitalization. Cox models generated hazard ratios (HRs); sensitivity analyses extended follow-up to 2-3 years with landmark analyses. RESULTS: At 1 year, GLP-1RA use was associated with significantly lower incident AF (1.63% vs. 2.62%; HR 0.655, 95% CI 0.508-0.844; p = 0.001), a 34.5% relative risk reduction. Reductions were consistent for paroxysmal (HR 0.586), persistent (HR 0.482), and longstanding persistent AF (HR 0.527; all p < 0.05); permanent AF was non-significant at 1 year. Ventricular tachyarrhythmias (HR 0.556; p = 0.002) and hospitalization (HR 0.668; p < 0.001) were also significantly reduced. Benefits persisted across 2- and 3-year follow-up and remained consistent in landmark analyses. CONCLUSIONS: In non-diabetic adults with obesity and CKD, GLP-1RA therapy was associated with significant, durable reductions in incident AF, ventricular arrhythmias, and hospitalization. These findings are consistent with a possible cardiorenal benefit and support prioritizing this phenotype for dedicated randomized trials.

方法

结果

结论与意义

在非糖尿病肥胖合并CKD成人中，GLP-1RA治疗与1年新发房颤、室性心律失常及住院的显著且持久下降相关，提示可能存在心肾获益。本研究为回顾性观察性设计，存在残余混杂可能，结果支持将该表型人群优先纳入专门的随机对照试验加以验证。

Investigating Factors Associated with the Occurrence of Atrial Fibrillation after Non-cardiothoracic Surgery.

Adv Biomed Res·2026-10-17·PMID 42891485
房颤与消融 Journal Article

Alireza Nematollahi、Parvin Bahrami、Mahshid Farshidnejad

英文摘要

BACKGROUND: Although the occurrence of atrial fibrillation (AF) after cardiac and thoracic surgery is common and leads to serious complications such as patients' vital organ dysfunction, pulmonary edema, myocardial infarction, and death, evidence about the occurrence and risk factors associated with post-operative atrial fibrillation (POAF) as new AF immediately after non-cardiac thoracic surgery is limited. Therefore, the present study aimed at investigating the factors related to the occurrence of POAF after non-cardiothoracic surgery. MATERIALS AND METHODS: The present retrospective study was conducted on 272 patients undergoing non-cardiothoracic surgeries during 2007-2020. Patients' basic and clinical information including age, sex, BMI, addiction, comorbidities, history of non-cardiac thoracic surgery, history of cardiac surgery, history of chronic kidney disease (CKD), history of ischemic heart disease (IHD), history of heart failure (HF), valvular heart disease (VHD), and the occurrence or non-occurrence of POAF was extracted from their records and then documented. RESULTS: In the present study, 48.1% and 51.9% of patients were with and without POAF, respectively. Risk factors associated with increasing the odds of POAF occurrence included age (OR = 1.094), anemia (OR = 3.947), IHD (OR = 4.23), CKD (OR = 4.765), VHD (OR = 4.781), pulmonary diseases (OR = 7.828), hypertension (HTN) (OR = 8.336), HF (OR = 11.790), and post-thoracic surgery (OR = 49.072) (P-value < 0.05). CONCLUSION: According to the results of the present study, aging, comorbidities (i.e., anemia, pulmonary diseases, HTN, and CKD), heart diseases (i.e., IHD, VHD, and HF), and post-thoracic surgery were among the most significant risk factors associated with the increased occurrence of POAF.

Totally Thoracoscopic Atrial Fibrillation Ablation With the Gemini S Bipolar Clamp: A Single-Center Descriptive Cohort Study of 1-Year Rhythm, Safety, and Quality-of-Life Outcomes.

房颤与消融 Journal Article

Michal Gruszczynski、Hector Rodriguez Cetina Biefer、Laura Rings 等 6 位作者

英文摘要

BACKGROUND: Outcome data for the Gemini S bipolar radiofrequency clamp are almost absent; the only indexed report being a three-patient case series. We describe 1-year outcomes in a consecutive cohort. METHODS: We retrospectively analyzed all consecutive patients treated with the Gemini S clamp between January 2021 and August 2024. Epicardial box isolation was combined with left atrial appendage (LAA) exclusion; completeness was confirmed by entry and exit block on epicardial three-dimensional mapping. The primary outcome was freedom from AF recurrence at 12 months, on and off antiarrhythmic drugs (AADs) after a 90-day blanking period. RESULTS: Twenty-three patients were included (mean age 64.4 ± 8.0 years; 82.6% male). AF was paroxysmal in 34.8%, persistent in 26.1%, and long-standing persistent in 39.1%; 60.9% had prior catheter ablation and 69.6% had failed or were intolerant to a Class I or III agent. A complete block-verified box lesion was achieved in 95.7% and LAA exclusion in 91.3%. Three patients (13.0%) required conversion to sternotomy for bleeding, exceeding larger series. There was no death, stroke, phrenic nerve palsy, or pacemaker implantation. At 1 year, 87.0% (95% CI 67.9-95.5) were in sinus rhythm, all off Class I/III AADs. CONCLUSIONS: Sinus rhythm was achieved in 87.0% of patients at 1 year. As the largest reported experience with this device, the series provides device-level evidence where little existed, but it is descriptive, not powered for comparison, and the conversion rate needs confirmation.

Localized bigeminal activity in the left atrial posterior wall preceding atrial fibrillation initiation.

J Cardiol Cases·2026-10-17·PMID 42889928
房颤与消融 Case Reports Journal Article

Ko Abe、Kazuhiro Nagaoka、Yasushi Mukai 等 6 位作者

英文摘要

Bigeminal activity of the pulmonary veins is considered an indicator of arrhythmogenic triggers of atrial fibrillation (AF). However, few reports have described localized bigeminal activity originating from non-pulmonary vein regions. We observed the phenomenon of local bigeminal activities, which did not develop into premature atrial contraction, in a restricted area of left atrial posterior wall (LAPW), preceding the initiation of the AF. Radiofrequency ablation targeting the site of these bigeminal activities transiently suppressed AF, and subsequent linear ablation connecting the site to the adjacent low-voltage region resulted in durable elimination of AF. 英文摘要

Cardiac re-entrant arrhythmias are a major cause of sudden cardiac death. Optogenetic defibrillation has emerged as a promising anti-arrhythmic strategy based on photoactivation of channelrhodopsins, yet the efficacy and potential adverse consequences of activating different classes of light-gated ion channels have not been systematically evaluated. Previous studies assessed either cation (e.g. ChR2) or anion (e.g. GtACR1) non-selective channels, both of which depolarize resting cardiomyocytes. Recently identified light-gated K+ channels (e.g. WiChR) suppress cardiomyocyte activity while maintaining the membrane potential near its resting state, but their defibrillation potential remains unexplored. We used computational simulations to compare ChR2, GtACR1 and WiChR. Single-cell simulations showed that ChR2 and GtACR1 markedly increased diastolic intracellular Ca2+ (by 42.6% and 52.6%, respectively), whereas WiChR induced only minimal changes (by 4.0%), suggesting lower pro-arrhythmogenic risk. WiChR, however, slightly increased intracellular Na+ (by 15.1% vs. 0.1% and 3.4% for ChR2 and GtACR1), consistent with the residual Na+ permeability of currently available K+-conducting channelrhodopsins. Simulations in human ventricles and atria showed that GtACR1 most effectively terminated re-entrant arrhythmias at low light intensities, whereas WiChR achieved comparable efficacy at ≥5 mW/mm2. Complementary tissue-scale simulations revealed that defibrillation occurs through depolarization within the excitable gap followed by fast Na+ channel inactivation (ChR2 and partly GtACR1), or through reduced membrane resistance at sufficiently high light intensities (large-conductance channels GtACR1 and WiChR). Overall, we demonstrate that channelrhodopsin ion selectivity is a key determinant of both optogenetic defibrillation efficacy and its underlying mechanisms. TRANSLATIONAL PERSPECTIVE: Re-entrant arrhythmias are a leading cause of sudden cardiac death. Standard therapies, including the activation of implantable cardioverter-defibrillators, carry burdens such as pain and myocardial injury. Optogenetic defibrillation has been suggested as a potentially gentler alternative, replacing high-voltage electrical shocks by photoactivation of light-gated ion channels expressed in cardiac cells. Determining which light-gated ion channel optimally balances efficacy and adverse effects on cardiac electrophysiology is critical for clinical translation. We addressed this question using computational simulations and validation experiments in zebrafish hearts. We identify ion selectivity as a key determinant of both the success rate for arrhythmia termination and the underlying mechanisms: non-selective cation- and anion-conducting channels terminate re-entry efficiently while promoting intracellular Ca2+ accumulation, a plausible substrate for pro-arrhythmic side effects that could undermine therapeutic safety. K+-conducting channels largely avoid this pro-arrhythmic risk by holding cardiomyocytes near their resting potential, albeit at the cost of requiring transmural illumination, while also causing mild Na+ accumulation attributable to residual Na+ permeability. Together, these trade-offs define clear clinical implications: K+-conducting channels may provide the safest option for optogenetic defibrillation, particularly once variants with further enhanced K+ selectivity and red-shifted activation spectra for improved operation within blood-perfused myocardium become available. KEY POINTS: We use computational simulations to compare cation non-selective (ChR2), anion non-selective (GtACR1) and K+-selective (WiChR) channelrhodopsins for optogenetic termination of re-entrant arrhythmia. Single-cardiomyocyte simulations suggest that ChR2 and GtACR1 activation can cause progressive accumulation of intracellular Ca2+, which is minimized when using WiChR. Intracellular microelectrode recordings from hearts of transgenic adult zebrafish validate the simulated voltage dynamics seen with ChR2, GtACR1 or WiChR activation. Simulations of human left ventricles and atria indicate that GtACR1 is most effective in terminating re-entrant arrhythmia at low light intensities, whereas WiChR becomes similarly effective at higher intensities. Tissue-scale simulations indicate distinct defibrillation mechanisms: excitable gap extinction by de novo action potential initiation followed by inactivation of fast Na+ channels for depolarizing channelrhodopsins (ChR2, GtACR1), and reduction in membrane resistance for the large-conductance channels (GtACR1, WiChR), effectively clamping the membrane potential at each channel's reversal potential at high light levels.

折返性心律失常是心源性猝死的主要原因，ICD 放电带来疼痛与心肌损伤，光遗传学除颤被视为更温和的替代方案。既往研究仅评估了阳离子型（如 ChR2）与阴离子型（如 GtACR1）非选择性光门控通道，二者均使静息心肌细胞去极化，促心律失常风险不明；新发现的 K+ 选择性光门控通道（如 WiChR）在抑制心肌活动的同时维持膜电位接近静息水平，但其除颤潜力从未被系统评估，不同通道类别之间的疗效与安全性权衡缺乏证据。

方法

本研究为计算模拟研究，并以转基因成年斑马鱼心脏细胞内微电极记录作为验证。构建单心肌细胞、人心房与心室组织及组织尺度模型，比较三类通道蛋白：阳离子非选择性 ChR2、阴离子非选择性 GtACR1、K+ 选择性 WiChR。评估指标包括舒张期细胞内 Ca2+ 与 Na+ 浓度变化、不同光照强度（含 ≥5 mW/mm2）下折返性心律失常的终止率，以及除颤的膜电生理机制（可兴奋间隙内去极化后快 Na+ 通道失活、膜电阻降低）。摘要未报告具体样本量与统计检验方法。

结果

单细胞模拟显示，ChR2 与 GtACR1 使舒张期细胞内 Ca2+ 分别升高 42.6% 与 52.6%，WiChR 仅升高 4.0%，提示促心律失常风险更低；但 WiChR 使细胞内 Na+ 升高 15.1%，而 ChR2 与 GtACR1 分别为 0.1% 与 3.4%，与现有 K+ 导通通道蛋白残余 Na+ 通透性一致。人心室与心房模拟中，GtACR1 在低光强下终止折返最有效，WiChR 在 ≥5 mW/mm2 时达到相当疗效。组织尺度模拟提示机制不同：ChR2 及部分 GtACR1 通过在可兴奋间隙内启动新动作电位继而快 Na+ 通道失活实现除颤；大电导通道（GtACR1、WiChR）在高光强下通过降低膜电阻、将膜电位钳制于各通道反转电位而起效。斑马鱼心脏微电极记录验证了三种通道激活时的模拟电压动态。

结论与意义

光敏通道蛋白的离子选择性是光遗传学除颤疗效及其机制的关键决定因素：非选择性阳离子与阴离子通道终止折返效率高，但促进细胞内 Ca2+ 蓄积，构成潜在促心律失常安全隐患；K+ 导通通道基本规避该风险，代价是需要透壁光照并伴轻度 Na+ 蓄积。就临床转化而言，K+ 选择性通道可能是最安全的光遗传学除颤选择，未来若获得 K+ 选择性更强、激活光谱红移（利于血液灌注心肌内操作）的变体将更具前景。局限性在于结论主要来自计算模拟与斑马鱼验证，尚缺乏人体在体验证，最佳光强与安全性需进一步实验确认。

Pathogenic Variants in NKX2-5 Associated With Familial Atrial Septal Defect and Conduction System Disease.

英文摘要

BACKGROUND: Most atrial septal defects (ASDs) occur sporadically, but familial cases can occur and carry a 10% risk of transmission to offspring. CASE SUMMARY: We describe a 3-generation family with autosomal dominant secundum ASD and conduction system disease related to a novel NKX2-5 variant (c.300del; p.Asp101Thrfs∗75). The index patient, a 58-year-old woman with prior ASD repair and mitral valve replacement, presented with chronotropic incompetence, atrial fibrillation, left bundle branch block, and prolonged pauses, leading to pacemaker implantation. Her mother had an ASD, atrial fibrillation, left bundle branch block, and pulmonary arterial hypertension. The proband's daughter also had an ASD. Genetic testing revealed a novel NKX2-5 pathogenic variant in all 3 patients. REVIEW SUMMARY: The combination of an ASD and conduction system disease represents the most common phenotype in patients with pathogenic NKX2-5 variants. TAKE-HOME MESSAGE: A thorough family history and genetic testing are needed when evaluating patients with ASD and concurrent conduction system abnormalities.

Successful extravascular implantable cardioverter-defibrillator implantation in a patient with short QT syndrome: A case report.

Short QT syndrome (SQTS) can present with life-threatening ventricular fibrillation (VF), requiring timely resuscitation and secondary prevention. A male patient in his 20s was resuscitated from VF and diagnosed with SQTS. Genetic testing may identify rare variants, such as in SLC4A3, expanding the known genetic spectrum of SQTS.
