Management of Post-heart Transplant Atrial Fibrillation

Arrhythm Electrophysiol Rev·2026-09-22·PMID 42866112
房颤与消融 Review Journal Article

Abdou M、Sifuentes AA、Goldar G 等 5 位作者

英文摘要

AF is the most common atrial arrhythmia encountered after orthotopic heart transplantation, with distinct pathophysiology and clinical implications. While early postoperative AF is typically transient, late-onset AF is increasingly recognised as a marker of graft dysfunction, rejection or vasculopathy. Risk factors include advanced recipient age, prolonged ischaemic time and immunosuppressive therapy. The denervated status of the transplanted heart alters typical electrophysiology, complicating diagnosis and management. Management requires individualised strategies balancing rate versus rhythm control, careful drug interaction monitoring and thromboembolic risk assessment. Emerging evidence supports catheter ablation in select cases, although data remain limited. Immunosuppressants, particularly calcineurin inhibitors and azathioprine, may increase AF susceptibility. Given the lack of transplant-specific AF guidelines, a multidisciplinary approach is essential. Improved understanding of AF in orthotopic heart transplantation is critical to optimising graft longevity and patient survival. Future studies are needed to guide evidence-based management and surveillance.

Dual antithrombotic therapy with dabigatran and ticagrelor in patients with acute coronary syndrome and non-valvular atrial fibrillation undergoing percutaneous coronary intervention (ADONIS-PCI): protocol for a randomized controlled pilot trial.

Cardiol J·2026-09-22·PMID 42770881
房颤与消融 Journal Article Clinical Trial Protocol

Miłosz J Jaguszewski、Aleksandra Gąsecka-van der Pol、Natasza Gilis-Malinowska 等 35 位作者

英文摘要

INTRODUCTION: Patients with atrial fibrillation (AF) presenting with acute coronary syndrome (ACS) and undergoing percutaneous coronary intervention (PCI) require protection against both cardioembolic and coronary thrombotic events, while facing a substantial bleeding risk. Oral anticoagulation (OAC) reduces the risk of stroke and systemic embolism but does not adequately address stent-related thrombotic risk. Conversely, dual antiplatelet therapy reduces recurrent coronary ischemic events and stent thrombosis but is insufficient for AF-related thromboembolism. Current antithrombotic strategies therefore rely on abbreviated triple antithrombotic therapy followed by dual antithrombotic therapy; however, the optimal P2Y12 inhibitor and dosing strategy in patients with AF and ACS treated with PCI remain uncertain. MATERIAL AND METHODS: ADONIS-PCI is an investigator-initiated, prospective, multicenter, randomized, open-label, blindedendpoint, non-inferiority trial (NCT04695106; EUCT number: 2024-515056-20-00). Consecutive patients with nonvalvular AF hospitalized for ACS and successfully treated with PCI are randomized within 120 hours after PCI in a 1:1 ratio to receive either dual antithrombotic therapy with dabigatran (150 mg twice daily or 110 mg twice daily) plus ticagrelor (90 mg twice daily for 1 month, followed by 60 mg twice daily up to 12 months), or standard antithrombotic therapy according to contemporary guideline-based care, consisting of dabigatran (150 mg twice daily or 110 mg twice daily), clopidogrel (75 mg once daily), and aspirin (75 mg once daily), followed by dual therapy according to bleeding and ischemic risk. Study treatment is continued for 12 months. The primary endpoint is the first International Society on Thrombosis and Haemostasis (ISTH) major or clinically relevant non-major bleeding event in a time-to-event analysis. The main secondary endpoint is a composite of thromboembolic events (myocardial infarction, stroke, or systemic embolism), death, or unplanned revascularization (PCI or coronary artery bypass grafting) at 12 months. ADONIS-PCI will evaluate whether a dabigatran-based aspirin-free strategy using ticagrelor dose de-escalation can preserve ischemic protection while reducing the bleeding burden in patients with AF and ACS undergoing PCI. CONCLUSIONS: ADONIS-PCI is among the first randomized evaluations of a reduced-dose long-term ticagrelor strategy combined with dabigatran in patients with AF and ACS undergoing PCI. The trial addresses an important evidence gap in the selection and dosing of P2Y12 inhibition when aspirin is discontinued early in this high-risk population.

Serum Potassium and Mineralocorticoid Receptor Antagonists As Modifiers of Atrial Arrhythmia Recurrence After Catheter Ablation for Atrial Fibrillation: A Narrative Review With Systematic Search and Mechanistic Synthesis.

Cureus·2026-09-22·PMID 42770069
房颤与消融 Journal Article Review

Shunichiro Tomita、Takuya Kishi

英文摘要

Atrial arrhythmia recurrence after catheter ablation for atrial fibrillation (AF) remains common despite advances in pulmonary vein isolation, mapping, lesion delivery, and rhythm monitoring. Contemporary guidelines emphasize integrated, risk-factor-based care, yet the roles of serum potassium management and mineralocorticoid receptor antagonist (MRA) therapy after ablation remain poorly defined. We aimed to synthesize the clinical and mechanistic evidence on whether serum potassium, potassium-management strategies, and MRA therapy (steroidal: spironolactone, eplerenone; nonsteroidal: esaxerenone, finerenone) modify recurrence, and to derive a testable mechanistic framework. Considering these backgrounds, we conducted a narrative review supported by a systematic literature search, prepared with reference to the PRISMA 2020 principles for transparency of search and selection and, because clinical heterogeneity precluded meta-analysis, the Synthesis Without Meta-analysis (SWiM) guideline. PubMed/MEDLINE and public professional society repositories were searched from inception to 6 June 2026, supplemented by hand searching; eligibility was prespecified using a PICOS framework. Study selection and extraction were performed in duplicate. The risk of bias was assessed using RoB 2 (randomized trials) and ROBINS-I (non-randomized studies). Findings were synthesized narratively across a prespecified evidence hierarchy (direct, indirect, mechanistic). Direct evidence was limited to five studies and was constrained by heterogeneity, small sample sizes in the MRA studies, and moderate-to-serious risk of bias in most non-randomized comparisons. One registry cohort (n = 4,838) reported a U-shaped association between preprocedural potassium and one-year recurrence, with the lowest observed risk at 4.41-4.60 mmol/L; however, this observational association does not establish an optimal therapeutic target or benefit from supplementation. Among four small/heterogeneous MRA studies, eplerenone and a single-center spironolactone randomized trial showed favorable recurrence signals, whereas retrospective esaxerenone showed no recurrence reduction despite improvements in secondary biomarker and remodeling measures; another study used renal sympathetic denervation as an active comparator and therefore did not establish MRA benefit over usual care. These findings should be interpreted cautiously because of study limitations, imprecision, confounding by indication, and variable recurrence ascertainment. Indirect evidence from non-ablation populations and mechanistic studies supports biological plausibility for interactions among potassium homeostasis, mineralocorticoid signaling, renal function, and atrial remodeling, but cannot validate a post-ablation treatment strategy. Accordingly, the proposed ionic-renal-aldosterone-fibrosis axis should be regarded as a hypothesis-generating framework rather than a clinically validated mechanism. Current evidence supports avoiding hypokalemia and hyperkalemia, correcting reversible disturbances, and prescribing MRAs for established indications with appropriate monitoring; it does not justify routine potassium supplementation or MRA use solely to prevent post-ablation recurrence. Prospective, adequately powered randomized studies with longitudinal potassium assessment and rigorous rhythm monitoring are required before mechanism-based post-ablation interventions can be recommended.

Impact of differences in kidney function estimation by creatinine compared to cystatin C on clinical outcomes in patients with atrial fibrillation

Eur Heart J Open·2026-09-22·PMID 42846172
房颤与消融 Journal Article

Krisai P、Mutti M、Aeschbacher S 等 17 位作者

英文摘要

Aims Differences between cystatin C-based and creatinine-based glomerular filtration rate estimations (eGFRdiff) have been associated with adverse outcomes. We aimed to investigate the prevalence of eGFRdiff and its association with clinical outcomes and brain lesions in patients with atrial fibrillation (AF).Methods and results We analysed 3804 patients with AF enrolled in two prospective, multicentre cohort studies. The eGFRdiff was calculated by 'cystatin C-based eGFR-creatinine-based eGFR'. Patients were stratified into three groups: eGFRdiff >10, -10 to 10, and <-10 mL/min/1.73 m². Associations with high-sensitivity troponin T (hs-TnT), NT-proBNP, major adverse cardiovascular (CV) events (stroke/systemic embolism, myocardial infarction and CV death), first heart failure (HF) hospitalization, and magnetic resonance imaging (MRI)-detected brain lesions were assessed. The mean age was 71 years, 28% were female, and 49% had paroxysmal AF. An eGFRdiff of >10, -10 to 10, and <-10 mL/min/1.73 m2 was present in 17.7%, 71.7%, and 10.6% of the patients, respectively. Incidence rates per 100 patient-years increased across these groups for major CV events (MACEs: 1.7, 3.9, 6.8) and HF hospitalization (1.2, 3.5, 6.1). Similarly, the prevalence of MRI-detected brain infarcts (28%, 39%, 42%) and moderate-to-severe white matter hyperintensities (38%, 55%, 63%) increased with greater eGFRdiff. The levels of hs-TnT and NT-proBNP were inversely correlated with eGFRdiff. In multivariable-adjusted linear models, lower eGFRdiff was associated with MACE (P = 0.006) and HF hospitalization (P = 0.015).Conclusion A substantial proportion of patients with AF exhibit clinically relevant eGFRdiff. More negative eGFR difference was associated with elevated cardiac biomarkers, greater burden of ischaemic brain lesions, and increased risk of CV events and HF hospitalization.Clinicaltrialsgov identifier NCT02105844.

JACC Clin Electrophysiol·2026-09-22·PMID 42820874
遗传与基础 Journal Article

Aydemir MM、Nalbant M、Karacan M 等 15 位作者

英文摘要

Background Catecholaminergic polymorphic ventricular tachycardia (CPVT) is a rare inherited arrhythmia with a high risk of life-threatening ventricular arrhythmias in childhood.Objectives This study sought to characterize pediatric CPVT and evaluate contemporary therapeutic outcomes, including left cardiac sympathetic denervation (LCSD) and bilateral cardiac sympathetic denervation (BCSD).Methods This retrospective multicenter study included 92 pediatric patients with CPVT from 3 tertiary centers in Türkiye between 2012 and 2025. Arrhythmic events included aborted cardiac arrest, sudden cardiac death, appropriate implantable cardioverter-defibrillator shocks, arrhythmic syncope, and ventricular tachycardia. Paired preprocedural and postprocedural event rates were compared in patients with an adequate preprocedural observation period.Results Median age at symptom onset was 9.25 years (IQR: 5.88-12.0), and median age at diagnosis was 11.6 years (IQR: 8.64-14.41). Among 87 genetically tested patients, 74 (85.1%) had a pathogenic or likely pathogenic variant; ryanodine receptor 2 and calsequestrin 2 variants were identified in 41 (47.1%) and 24 (27.6%), respectively. Fifty patients underwent sympathetic denervation (LCSD, n = 25; BCSD, n = 25). In the paired cohort (n = 35), median arrhythmic event rate decreased from 3.20 events/year (IQR: 1.74-8.76) before LCSD/BCSD to 0.00 events/year (IQR: 0.00-1.35) during the first 2 postprocedural years (P < 0.001). First 2-year event rates did not differ significantly between procedures. Seven patients died; documented treatment nonadherence was present in all.Conclusions LCSD/BCSD was associated with a substantial reduction in arrhythmic burden, but observational treatment allocation and center-specific management preclude conclusions regarding comparative effectiveness. Sustained treatment adherence remains essential.

研究动机

儿茶酚胺敏感性多形性室性心动过速（CPVT）是罕见的遗传性心律失常，儿童期即面临心脏性猝死高风险。既往证据多来自小样本或成人队列，儿童CPVT的基因型谱分布、现代治疗（尤其是左心交感神经去神经术LCSD与双侧交感神经去神经术BCSD）后的真实心律失常负荷变化及长期结局仍缺乏系统描述，临床决策缺少儿科专属数据。

方法

研究类型：回顾性多中心队列研究。研究对象：2012—2025年土耳其3家三级中心的92例儿童CPVT患者。暴露/干预：交感神经去神经术（LCSD 25例、BCSD 25例，共50例）。终点：心律失常事件复合终点，包括复苏成功的心脏骤停、心脏性猝死、ICD恰当放电、心律失常性晕厥及室性心动过速。统计方法：对具备充分术前观察期的患者进行术前与术后事件率配对比较（配对队列 n=35），并比较LCSD与BCSD术后前2年的事件率。

结果

症状首发中位年龄9.25岁（IQR 5.88–12.0），诊断中位年龄11.6岁（IQR 8.64–14.41）。87例接受基因检测者中74例（85.1%）携带致病或可能致病变异，RYR2变异41例（47.1%）、CASQ2变异24例（27.6%）。配对队列（n=35）心律失常事件率由术前3.20次/年（IQR 1.74–8.76）降至术后前2年的0.00次/年（IQR 0.00–1.35），P<0.001；LCSD与BCSD术后前2年事件率差异无统计学意义。共7例死亡，全部存在记录在案的治疗依从性不佳。

结论与意义

在儿童CPVT队列中，LCSD/BCSD与心律失常负荷显著下降相关，提示对药物难治性高危患儿可作为重要的辅助治疗手段；但治疗分配为观察性、各中心管理策略存在差异，且缺乏对照，故不能就LCSD与BCSD的比较疗效得出结论。7例死亡者均存在依从性问题，强调持续的治疗依从性与随访管理是改善预后的关键。局限性包括回顾性设计、样本量小、事件率配对分析仅覆盖部分患者及单国家多中心数据，外推需谨慎。

Mapping publication trends in oral anticoagulation in atrial fibrillation: A bibliometric and thematic analysis of 11,039 publications (2006-2025).

North Clin Istanb·2026-09-22·PMID 42769560
遗传与基础 Journal Article

Senol Coskun、Erkan Tiyekli

英文摘要

OBJECTIVE: This study aimed to systematically map the intellectual evolution, thematic development, and collaborative patterns of oral anticoagulation research in atrial fibrillation (AF) from 2006 to 2025. METHODS: This bibliometric study analyzed 11,039 documents indexed in the Web of Science Core Collection (2006-2025) using the Bibliometrix R package (version 4.x) within the R statistical environment (version 4.3.1) for comprehensive science mapping. The analytical workflow comprised descriptive performance analysis, collaboration network analysis, keyword co-occurrence analysis, strategic thematic mapping, and latent Dirichlet allocation (LDA) topic modeling. RESULTS: Publication output increased exponentially from 2 articles per year (2006) to a peak of 960 per year (2021), with subsequent stabilization that may reflect consolidation of the field. The United States produced the highest number of publications (3636; 32.9%). The RE-LY trial received the most citations (n=8788). Thematic mapping revealed two main research clusters: Motor themes (141 keywords), representing direct oral anticoagulant agents, stroke prevention, and clinical outcomes; and emerging/declining themes (43 keywords), including venous thromboembolism and pharmacokinetics. LDA topic modeling identified nine distinct research domains encompassing real-world data, safety reporting, and implementation guidelines. CONCLUSION: The bibliometric patterns observed are consistent with a maturing research field, characterized by a gradual shift from efficacy-focused randomized trials toward real-world effectiveness studies, implementation science, and personalized anticoagulation strategies. Future research may benefit from increased emphasis on long-term adherence, health-economic outcomes, and personalized approaches integrating genetic, clinical, and patient-centered factors.

研究动机

房颤口服抗凝（包括直接口服抗凝药 DOAC 与华法林）是卒中预防的基石，但该领域文献体量庞大且分散，既往综述多为定性叙述，缺乏对研究主题演化、协作网络与知识结构的系统量化描绘，也难以判断哪些主题已成熟、哪些正在兴起或衰退。本研究旨在填补这一空白，为后续研究方向与指南落地提供证据地图。

方法

文献计量学研究。数据源为 Web of Science 核心合集 2006–2025 年索引的 11,039 篇文献；在 R 4.3.1 环境中使用 Bibliometrix R 包（4.x 版）完成科学知识图谱分析。分析流程包括描述性绩效分析、合作网络分析、关键词共现分析、战略性主题图谱（strategic thematic mapping）以及潜在狄利克雷分配（LDA）主题建模。研究未设传统临床终点，主要观察指标为发文量趋势、国家/机构贡献、被引频次与主题聚类结构；摘要未报告任何效应量、95%CI 或 P 值。

结果

年发文量从 2006 年的每年 2 篇呈指数增长至 2021 年峰值每年 960 篇，此后趋于稳定，提示领域进入整合期。美国发文量最高（3,636 篇，占 32.9%）。被引最多的单篇研究为 RE-LY 试验（被引 8,788 次）。战略性主题图谱显示两大聚类：一是“驱动型主题”（motor themes，141 个关键词），涵盖 DOAC 药物、卒中预防与临床结局；二是新兴/衰退主题（43 个关键词），涉及静脉血栓栓塞症与药代动力学。LDA 主题建模识别出 9 个研究域，包括真实世界数据、安全性报告与实施指南。摘要未报告各聚类的密度、中心度具体数值及统计检验结果。

结论与意义

上述文献计量学特征与“研究领域日趋成熟”的判断一致：研究重心由以疗效为目标的随机对照试验逐步转向真实世界有效性研究、实施科学与个体化抗凝策略。对电生理与房颤管理实践而言，提示未来证据增量将更多来自注册数据库、安全性监测与指南落地环节；后续研究可加强长期依从性、卫生经济学结局，以及整合遗传、临床与以患者为中心因素的个体化路径。局限性在于：仅纳入 Web of Science 单一数据库，存在收录偏倚与引文滞后；文献计量指标不能反映研究质量；LDA 主题划分依赖关键词与算法参数设定，具有一定主观性。
