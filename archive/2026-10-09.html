Cost-Effectiveness of Annual Atrial Fibrillation Screening Compared with Current Opportunistic Screening in Australian Primary Care: A Model-Based Economic Evaluation.

Appl Health Econ Health Policy·2026-10-09·PMID 42853363
房颤与消融 Journal Article

Ureni Halahakone、Sameera Senanayake、Victoria McCreanor 等 5 位作者

英文摘要

BACKGROUND: Although the guidelines recommend opportunistic screening for adults ≥ 65 years, screening for atrial fibrillation (AF) remains underutilised in Australia. The absence of guidance on how often to screen has resulted in variable practice and missed opportunities for AF detection. Previous studies have focused on handheld devices rather than 12-lead electrocardiogram (ECG) devices that are readily available in primary-care settings, and the evidence supporting an annual AF screening programme in Australia is limited. Therefore, the aim of this study is to compare the cost-effectiveness of an annual, structured, higher‑coverage opportunistic ECG screening approach (annual screening) with current unstructured opportunistic screening (current screening) in older adults in Australia. METHODS: A Markov model was developed to evaluate two strategies for adults ≥ 65 years: (1) annual screening using 12-lead ECG and (2) current opportunistic screening. The model included 11 health states capturing AF treatment and outcomes, and simulations were run using monthly cycles. A health system perspective and 10-year time horizon were applied. To account for uncertainty, a probabilistic sensitivity analysis with 1000 iterations was undertaken and the results were assessed against a cost-effectiveness threshold of 28,000 Australian dollars (AU$) per quality-adjusted life year (QALY). Uncertainty was further examined through cost-effectiveness acceptability curves and scenario analyses to test the stability of findings under alternative assumptions. RESULTS: Annual screening increased costs by AU$4213 and gained 0.43 QALYs, yielding an incremental cost-effectiveness ratio of AU$9797 per QALY gained. Probabilistic sensitivity analysis demonstrated that 100% of simulations yielded a positive incremental net monetary benefit. Scenario analyses demonstrated that both higher screening costs and less frequent 3-yearly screening were cost-effective in comparison to the current opportunistic screening, with incremental cost-effectiveness ratios below the cost-effectiveness threshold and positive incremental net monetary benefits in all scenarios. CONCLUSION: A structured annual screening approach for AF is likely to be a cost-effective strategy within the Australian primary-care setting. These findings highlight the benefits of more consistent screening practices for early AF detection and stroke prevention.

结果

与现行机会性筛查相比，年度筛查人均增加成本4213澳元，同时增加0.43个QALY，增量成本效果比（ICER）为9797澳元/QALY。概率敏感性分析中，100%的模拟结果显示增量净货币效益为正。情景分析显示，即使采用更高的筛查成本假设，或将筛查间隔改为每3年一次，ICER仍低于28000澳元/QALY的阈值，且所有情景下增量净货币效益均为正。摘要未报告房颤检出率、卒中或出血事件数等临床终点数据。

结论与意义

在澳大利亚基层医疗环境下，对≥65岁人群实施结构化年度房颤筛查很可能是具有成本效果的策略，提示更一致的筛查实践有助于早期发现房颤并预防卒中。局限性在于：结论来自模型模拟而非头对头随机试验，其稳健性依赖于模型假设与外部参数来源；情景分析仅覆盖筛查成本与筛查间隔，未涉及抗凝依从性、检出后治疗获益等关键假设的不确定性，且未报告临床终点，外推至其他医疗体系时需谨慎。

Oral Obesity Medicines in the Era of Cardiovascular-Kidney-Metabolic Syndrome (CKM): Beyond Weight Loss Toward Disease Modification

Drug Des Devel Ther·2026-10-09·PMID 42871841
房颤与消融 Review Journal Article

Alhazmi A、Le Roux CW

英文摘要

Obesity is a major driver of Cardiovascular-Kidney-Metabolic (CKM) syndrome and contributes to the development of atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease, heart failure, atrial fibrillation, chronic kidney disease, and type 2 diabetes. Historically, oral obesity pharmacotherapy was evaluated primarily according to weight loss efficacy. However, the emergence of the CKM framework has shifted therapeutic priorities toward modifying obesity related complications and improving long term clinical outcomes. This review summarizes currently approved oral obesity medications, including first generation agents (orlistat, phentermine/topiramate, and naltrexone/bupropion) and second generation incretin-based therapies (oral semaglutide and orforglipron), within the context of CKM syndrome. First generation agents provide modest-to-moderate weight loss and variable metabolic benefits, but evidence supporting cardiovascular or kidney outcome improvement remains limited and largely observational. In contrast, oral incretin-based therapies demonstrate broader effects across the CKM spectrum, including substantial weight reduction, improved glycemic control, favorable effects on blood pressure and triglycerides, and emerging kidney benefits. Among currently available oral agents, oral semaglutide has the strongest evidence, with the SOUL trial demonstrating a reduction in major adverse cardiovascular events in high-risk individuals with type 2 diabetes. Signals suggesting benefits in heart failure and atrial fibrillation are encouraging but remain exploratory. Orforglipron represents the first non-peptide oral glucagon-like peptide-1 receptor agonist and offers simplified administration with promising metabolic efficacy; however, cardiovascular, kidney, heart failure, and arrhythmia outcome data are still awaited. Collectively, the role of oral obesity pharmacotherapy is evolving from weight management alone toward comprehensive CKM care.

ERRATUM: Beyond the classical triangle: morphological variations of the Triangle of Koch in human hearts and their clinical implications.

Folia Morphol (Warsz)·2026-10-09·PMID 42851158
房颤与消融 Journal Article

Apurba Patra、Rajiv Kumar、Vandana Tiwari 等 10 位作者

英文摘要

BACKGROUND: The Triangle of Koch (TOK) is an important anatomical region located in the right atrium of the heart. It contains the atrioventricular (AV) node and forms a crucial landmark during electrophysiological and cardiac surgical procedures. Variations in its boundaries and morphology may increase the risk of inadvertent injury to the cardiac conduction system. The present study aimed to evaluate the morphology (shape, boundaries, and anatomical variations) of the TOK in thirty donated human hearts and its clinical implications. MATERIALS AND METHODS: Thirty formalin-fixed adult human hearts were studied in the Department of Anatomy. The right atrium was opened carefully to expose the TOK. The boundaries formed by the tendon of Todaro (TT), the septal leaflet of the tricuspid valve, and the ostium of the coronary sinus (CS) were identified. Morphological features, location of the apex, and anatomical variations were documented and photographed. RESULTS: The TOK was identified in all specimens. Considerable variations were observed in shape, prominence of the TT, and position of the CS ostium. In most cases, it was broad and triangular, while in some hearts, the triangle appeared narrow and elongated. The apex corresponding to the AV nodal region showed positional variation relative to the membranous septum. Variability in the septal leaflet attachment and CS morphology was also noted. CONCLUSIONS: The TOK demonstrates significant anatomical variation in its shape and boundaries. Knowledge of these variations is essential for cardiologists, electrophysiologists, and cardiac surgeons during catheter ablation, pacemaker implantation, and valve surgeries to prevent injury to the AV node and associated conduction pathways.

Substrate Comprising Fibrosis and Epicardial Adipose Tissue-Associated Electrophysiological Remodeling Can Sustain Atrial Fibrillation Drivers: Insights from Image-Personalized Computational Simulations.

Heart Rhythm·2026-10-09·PMID 42854885
房颤与消融 Journal Article

Yaacoub Chahine、Heather Wood、Åshild Telle 等 12 位作者

英文摘要

BACKGROUND: Computational models of atrial fibrillation (AF) help clarify arrhythmia mechanisms, but the contribution of left atrial (LA) epicardial adipose tissue (EAT) is rarely included. OBJECTIVE: To assess whether incorporating EAT-associated effects into fibrosis-based atrial models alters reentrant arrhythmia inducibility, and to characterize the pro-arrhythmic properties of EAT-affected tissue. METHODS: LA fibrosis and EAT were quantified by LGE and Dixon MRI in 19 patients with persistent AF. For each patient, three anatomically identical image-personalized models were built (fibrosis only; fibrosis plus half-epicardial EAT effects, 0.5 mm; fibrosis plus full-epicardial EAT effects, 1.0 mm), yielding 855 simulations. In EAT regions, inward rectifier K+ current was reduced by 35%, intracellular K+ by 50%, and conduction velocity by 25%. RESULTS: Sustained reentry occurred in 370 simulations (43.3%), of which 306 had a functional (rotor-driven) component. Functional reentry inducibility rose from 22.8% (fibrosis only) to 41.4% (half-EAT) and 43.2% (full-EAT). At least one functional reentry was inducible in 12/19 fibrosis-only models, 18/19 half-EAT models, and 19/19 full-EAT models. Median inducible simulations per patient increased from 4 to 6 (p=0.004) and 7 (p=0.003), without significant difference between half- and full-EAT (p=0.895). Fibrosis/EAT overlap regions comprised only 2.1% of the LA wall but were the most arrhythmogenic, with a median 65.5% of their volume harboring reentry versus 8.8% (non-substrate), 37.3% (fibrosis), and 42.8% (EAT) (all p<0.001). In an exploratory analysis of PVI-only ablation patients (n=13), fibrosis-EAT overlap outside the PVI region was associated with 1-year recurrence (log-rank p=0.007). CONCLUSION: Adding EAT effects to fibrosis-based models increased sustained functional reentry, with transmural depth contributing only modestly. Reentry anchored preferentially where fibrosis and EAT effects coexisted.

Nuclear magnetic resonance-derived lipoprotein profile and risk of atrial tachyarrhythmia recurrence after catheter ablation: a case-cohort design within the PREDIMAR trial.

Heart Rhythm·2026-10-09·PMID 42854881
房颤与消融 Journal Article

Authors' Names Lucía Belzunce、Leticia Goni、M Teresa Barrio-López 等 16 位作者

英文摘要

BACKGROUND: Lipoproteins could be potential biomarkers of atrial tachyarrhythmia recurrence (TAR) after catheter ablation; however, their association remains inconclusive and based exclusively on routine lipid measurements. Nuclear magnetic resonance (NMR) enables detailed lipoprotein characterization beyond conventional assays. OBJECTIVE: To identify a baseline NMR-derived lipoprotein profile associated with the risk of TAR after catheter ablation. METHODS: We conducted a case-cohort study (212 cases and 238 participants in a random sub-cohort, with 93 overlapping cases) nested in the PREDIMAR (PRevención con DIeta Mediterránea de Arritmias Recurrentes) trial. Conventional lipoprotein profiles were measured using the Pentra C-200 and advanced lipoprotein profiles were assessed by NMR. Lipoprotein values were normalized and scaled to one standard deviation using Blom's inverse normal transformation. Cox models were used to assess the associations between baseline lipoproteins and TAR.
