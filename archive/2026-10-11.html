Pulsed field ablation with Renal denervation for persistent Atrial fibrIllation: Rationale and design of the PRAISE-AF randomized trial.

Am Heart J·2026-10-11·PMID 42860163
房颤与消融 Journal Article

Jiongchao Guo、Mu Qin、Weifeng Jiang 等 9 位作者

英文摘要

BACKGROUND: Atrial fibrillation (AF) is the most common sustained cardiac arrhythmia worldwide and is associated with increased risks of stroke, heart failure, hospitalization, disability, and mortality. Catheter ablation has become an established rhythm-control strategy for symptomatic AF; however, the recurrence rate after ablation remains substantial, especially in patients with persistent AF. Pulsed field ablation (PFA) is an emerging nonthermal ablation technology with myocardial tissue selectivity and a potentially improved safety profile compared with conventional thermal ablation. Renal denervation (RDN) reduces sympathetic nervous system activity and has shown promising effects in reducing AF recurrence when combined with catheter ablation, mainly in patients with paroxysmal AF and hypertension. Whether RDN combined with PFA improves rhythm outcomes in patients with persistent AF remains unknown. TRIAL DESIGN: This is a single center, prospective, randomized, patient-blinded, sham-controlled pilot exploratory trial. A total of 86 patients with symptomatic persistent AF undergoing first-time catheter ablation will be enrolled and randomly assigned in a 1:1 ratio to receive either PFA combined with RDN or PFA combined with a sham RDN procedure. Antiarrhythmic drugs (AADs) will be discontinued after the index procedure in both groups. The primary endpoint is freedom from documented atrial tachyarrhythmia recurrence, including AF, atrial flutter, or atrial tachycardia lasting more than 30 seconds, within 12 months after a single ablation procedure without AADs. Secondary endpoints include freedom from atrial tachycardia recurrence within 3 months, AF burden, blood pressure changes, and major adverse cardiovascular events. Safety endpoints include cardiac, vascular, renal, and other procedure-related adverse events. CONCLUSIONS: This trial will assess the safety and efficacy of PFA with RDN in a postablation contemporary persistent AF patient population. TRIAL REGISTRATION: ClinicalTrials.gov:NCT07320768.

方法

单中心、前瞻性、随机、患者盲法、假手术对照的探索性（pilot）试验，计划纳入 86 例接受首次导管消融的症状性持续性 AF 患者，按 1:1 随机分为 PFA 联合 RDN 组与 PFA 联合假 RDN 手术组；两组术后均停用抗心律失常药物（AAD）。主要终点为单次消融术后 12 个月内、不使用 AAD 条件下无记录到的房性快速性心律失常复发，定义为持续超过 30 秒的 AF、心房扑动或房性心动过速。次要终点包括术后 3 个月内无房速复发、AF 负荷、血压变化及主要不良心血管事件；安全性终点涵盖心脏、血管、肾脏及其他手术相关不良事件。试验注册号 ClinicalTrials.gov:NCT07320768。

结果

本摘要为研究设计论文，摘要未报告任何入组、随访或疗效/安全性数据，主要及次要终点的效应量与 95%CI、P 值均摘要未报告。

结论与意义

该试验将在此当代持续性 AF 消融人群中，评估 PFA 联合 RDN 的安全性与疗效，有望为持续性 AF 消融是否需要联合自主神经调节提供随机对照的初步证据。局限性方面：样本量仅 86 例、单中心、属探索性 pilot 设计，检验效能有限；患者盲法但对术者无法设盲；且主要终点采用 12 个月无 AAD 复发这一较严格的节律指标，结果外推至广泛持续性 AF 人群需谨慎，尚待更大规模多中心试验验证。

Longitudinal Left Atrial Remodeling With Pharmacological Rhythm Versus Rate Control in Paroxysmal and Persistent Atrial Fibrillation.

Echocardiography·2026-10-11·PMID 42859965
房颤与消融 Journal Article

Shao-Fu Chien、Chih-Hui Chin、Chao-Chien Chang 等 5 位作者

英文摘要

PURPOSE: Evidence regarding left atrial (LA) remodeling with pharmacological rhythm control remains limited, particularly in patients with atrial fibrillation (AF) outside heart-failure-specific populations. METHODS: This single-center retrospective cohort included 156 adults with paroxysmal or persistent AF receiving mutually exclusive pharmacological rate-control (n = 92) or rhythm-control (n = 64) therapy who underwent two transthoracic echocardiograms. LA remodeling was assessed using serial echocardiography, and clinical outcomes were evaluated after the second examination. RESULTS: Among 74 patients with paired left atrial volume index (LAVI) measurements, LAVI increased from 39.9 to 50.3 mL/m2 with rate control (p < 0.001) but remained unchanged with rhythm control (44.9 to 43.4 mL/m2; p = 0.465). After adjustment for baseline LAVI and clinical covariates, rhythm control was associated with a 9.38 mL/m2 lower follow-up LAVI (95% CI, -15.53 to -3.24; p = 0.003). The association with lower follow-up LAVI was also observed among patients without heart failure and remained similar in sensitivity analyses. During post-landmark follow-up, rhythm control was associated with a lower hazard of all-cause mortality or stroke hospitalization (adjusted HR, 0.46; 95% CI, 0.24-0.88; p = 0.019). CONCLUSION: Pharmacological rhythm control was associated with more favorable longitudinal LA remodeling than rate control, including among patients without heart failure, and was also associated with a lower hazard of subsequent all-cause mortality or stroke hospitalization.

Sleep-Disordered Breathing and Sleep Fragmentation in Relation to Atrial Fibrillation Risk: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis.

J Arrhythm·2026-10-11·PMID 42859517
房颤与消融 Journal Article

Ermias Sori、Hassaan Abid、Zartasha 等 10 位作者

英文摘要

BACKGROUND: Atrial fibrillation (AF) is one of the most prevalent cardiac arrhythmias worldwide. This study aimed to evaluate the association between sleep-disordered breathing (SDB), primarily assessed using the apnea-hypopnea index (AHI), and AF risk, with additional analysis of sleep fragmentation parameters such as wake after sleep onset (WASO) and arousal index (AI). METHODS: This systematic review and meta-analysis followed Cochrane Handbook recommendations and PRISMA guidelines, with protocol registration in PROSPERO (No. 127490). Randomized controlled trials (RCTs) and observational cohort studies assessing sleep disturbances and AF were included. MEDLINE, Cochrane Library, and Scopus were searched from inception to February 2026. Extracted data included study characteristics, demographics, cardiovascular risk factors, and sleep metrics. Outcomes were incident and recurrent AF. A random-effects model was applied. RESULTS: Twenty-one cohort studies (611,898 participants) were included. SDB was associated with increased incident AF risk (RR 1.67, 95% CI 1.41-1.97; I 2 = 66.5%), with consistent Bayesian estimates. A dose-response relationship showed a 2% increase in AF risk per unit rise in AHI. For AF recurrence, 11 studies (n = 1810) demonstrated a significant association (RR 1.55, 95% CI 1.40-1.72; I 2 = 0%), supported by Bayesian analysis. CONCLUSION: Sleep disturbances are significantly associated with both incident and recurrent AF and may represent modifiable risk factors.

结论与意义

睡眠障碍与新发房颤及复发房颤均显著相关，可能是可干预的房颤危险因素，提示在房颤患者（尤其拟行或已行导管消融者）中应常规筛查并治疗 SDB。局限性：纳入研究均为观察性队列，残余混杂无法排除；新发房颤分析异质性中等（I² = 66.5%）；睡眠碎片化指标证据不足，尚需随机对照试验验证治疗 SDB 能否降低房颤负荷与复发率。

Development and Validation of a Novel Contact Force-Integrated Pulsed Field Quantization Index for Pulmonary Vein Isolation in a Porcine Model.

J Cardiovasc Electrophysiol·2026-10-11·PMID 42860018
房颤与消融 Journal Article

Jie Yang、Yi Zhang、Jie Zheng 等 15 位作者

英文摘要

INTRODUCTION: There is no bipolar-specific quantitative ablation index currently available to guide pulsed field ablation (PFA). We aimed to develop and validate a novel contact force (CF)-integrated pulsed field quantization index (PFQI) for real-time lesion depth prediction during bipolar PFA. METHODS AND RESULTS: In total, 27 healthy Yorkshire swine were included in a two-phase preclinical study. In Phase I, a nonlinear multivariable model was developed to derive a novel PFQI incorporating real-time CF, pulse train number, delivery voltage, and impedance variation. In Phase II, the PFQI was prospectively validated by comparing predicted with actual lesion depth, followed by PFQI-guided circumferential pulmonary vein isolation (PVI). Acute procedural success, 30-day PVI durability, and extracardiac safety were evaluated using serial electroanatomic mapping and blinded histopathological examination. Lesion depth was strongly associated with CF, pulse train number, voltage, and impedance change. The PFQI predicted actual depth with high accuracy (R = 0.94, R2 = 0.88; validation R2 = 0.82, slope = 1.014). In the validation cohort, 92% of predicted depths fell within ±0.75 mm of measured values. PFQI-guided PVI achieved 100% (6/6) acute isolation and 100% 30-day durable electrical isolation, with contiguous transmural fibrosis on histology. No collateral phrenic nerve, esophageal, or pulmonary vein injury occurred, and phrenic capture thresholds remained unchanged. CONCLUSION: The PFQI is the first bipolar-specific, CF-integrated quantitative metric for PFA, enabling accurate, real-time lesion depth prediction. PFQI-guided PVI achieved consistent, durable, and safe PVI in a porcine model, providing a promising operator-independent quantitative strategy to optimize clinical bipolar PFA for atrial fibrillation.

PFQI是首个双极特异性、整合接触力的PFA定量指标，可实时准确预测消融灶深度；在猪模型中，以其指导的PVI实现了均一、持久且安全的隔离效果，显示出优化临床双极PFA、实现术者无关量化消融的潜力。局限性包括：仅为动物实验、PVI验证组样本量小（n=6）、随访仅30天，且尚无人体临床数据，需进一步临床研究验证其通用性与安全性。

Left Atrial Appendage Closure Versus Oral Anticoagulation for Prevention of Stroke or Systemic Embolism in Patients With Atrial Fibrillation: An Updated Meta-Analysis of 7004 Patients Across Six Randomized Trials.

J Cardiovasc Electrophysiol·2026-10-11·PMID 42860016
房颤与消融 Journal Article

Aymen Ahmed、Neha Saleem Paryani、Muteia Shakoor 等 10 位作者

英文摘要

INTRODUCTION: Left atrial appendage closure (LAAC) is an emerging alternative for stroke prevention in patients with atrial fibrillation (AF) who are unsuitable for long-term oral anticoagulants (OACs). However, its efficacy and safety relative to OACs remain uncertain. METHODS: Three databases were searched up to April 7, 2026, for randomized controlled trials comparing LAAC with OAC in patients with AF. Risk ratios with 95% confidence intervals were pooled using a random-effects model. RESULTS: Six RCTs (n = 7004 patients) were analyzed. The risk of stroke or systemic embolism (SSE) did not differ significantly between the LAAC and OAC groups (RR: 1.10 [0.87-1.39]; p = 0.42). The risk of ischemic SSE was significantly increased with LAAC (RR: 1.41 [1.07-1.85]; p = 0.02) compared with OAC. No significant differences were observed in all-cause mortality (RR: 0.94 [0.81-1.09]), cardiovascular mortality (RR: 0.91 [0.69-1.21]), non-cardiovascular mortality (RR: 0.91 [0.75-1.12]), or hemorrhagic stroke (RR: 0.58 [0.29-1.17]). LAAC was associated with a similar risk of overall major bleeding (RR: 0.92 [0.77-1.09]) and low risk of non-procedural bleeding (RR: 0.61 [0.52-0.73]; p < 0.001) compared with OAC. Upon sensitivity analysis comparing LAAC with DOACs, the findings remained consistent across most clinical outcomes. CONCLUSION: LAAC demonstrates comparable risks of SSE and major bleeding to OACs, but is associated with a higher risk of ischemic SSE and reduced non-procedural bleeding, supporting its use in selected patients at high risk for long-term bleeding while emphasizing individualized shared decision-making.

研究动机

左心耳封堵（LAAC）已成为不适合长期口服抗凝药（OAC）的房颤患者预防卒中的新兴替代方案，但其相对于OAC的有效性与安全性仍不明确，既往单项RCT样本量有限、终点定义不一，需要更新汇总证据来回答这一问题。

方法

系统检索三个数据库截至2026年4月7日的随机对照试验（RCT），纳入比较房颤患者LAAC与OAC的RCT，合并计算风险比（RR）及95%置信区间，采用随机效应模型；并对LAAC与DOAC比较进行敏感性分析。主要终点为卒中或体循环栓塞（SSE），次要终点包括缺血性SSE、全因/心血管/非心血管死亡、出血性卒中、大出血及非手术相关出血。

结果

英文摘要

Catecholaminergic polymorphic ventricular tachycardia (CPVT) is a genetic arrhythmic syndrome caused by mutations in the sarcoplasmic reticulum (SR) calcium (Ca2+) release channel RyR2 or its accessory proteins, including calsequestrin-2 (CASQ2). RyR2 dysfunction or leak is the hallmark feature of CPVT and directly triggers arrhythmias. Despite this electrical instability, CPVT hearts are typically anatomically normal and lack pathological remodelling. Therefore, we hypothesized that introducing additional stressors, such as metabolic stress, would worsen cardiac arrhythmias and potentially trigger pathological remodelling. To test this hypothesis, metabolic stress was introduced by crossbreeding a CPVT model of CASQ2-/- mice with the Akita mouse, a genetic model of diabetes that also lacks cardiac remodelling. We found that dysregulation of myocyte Ca2+ handling was exacerbated in Akita-CASQ2-/- cells, which exhibited slower Ca2+ transient decay kinetics and increased arrhythmogenic Ca2+ waves. Akita-CASQ2-/- mice also displayed more severe arrhythmias, diastolic dysfunction, and cardiac hypertrophy, but without evidence of fibrosis, cell death, or worsened metabolic dysfunction. Finally, mitochondrial reactive oxygen species production was increased in Akita-CASQ2-/- cardiomyocytes, which may contribute to the more severe pathological phenotype. Overall, our results suggest that metabolic stress can aggravate dysregulated myocyte Ca2+ handling and promote cardiac dysfunction in CPVT hearts.

英文摘要

Congenital long QT syndrome (LQTS), the first identified cardiac ion channelopathy, is characterized by delayed ventricular repolarization and a prolonged heart rate-corrected QT interval (QTc), conferring an increased risk of malignant ventricular arrhythmias and sudden cardiac death.
