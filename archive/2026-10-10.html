Body Mass Index Variability and Cardiovascular Risk: Contemporary Evidence and Clinical Implications for Diabetes Care

Curr Diab Rep·2026-10-10·PMID 42859309
房颤与消融 Review Journal Article

Kaze AD、Echouffo-Tcheugui JB

英文摘要

Purpose of review To synthesize contemporary evidence on body mass index (BMI) variability as a determinant of cardiovascular risk, integrate mechanistic insights, and clarify its clinical relevance in individuals with and without diabetes.Recent findings Large cohort studies demonstrate that greater BMI or weight variability is associated with increased risks of all-cause mortality, atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease, heart failure and atrial fibrillation, independent of mean BMI. Mechanistic evidence to explain the observed association implicates adipose tissue dysfunction, ectopic fat deposition, metabolic instability, systemic inflammation, and autonomic imbalance. Diabetes may amplify these pathways through impaired metabolic flexibility and treatment-related weight fluctuations. Evidence from diabetes-specific cohorts suggests that structured, intentional weight loss may attenuate the adverse associations seen with unstructured weight cycling. BMI variability is an emerging marker of cardiometabolic instability with implications for cardiovascular risk, particularly in diabetes. Defining actionable thresholds and determining whether stabilizing weight improves outcomes are key future research priorities.

结果

摘要未报告具体效应量、95% CI 与 P 值。定性汇总显示：较大的 BMI 或体重变异性与全因死亡、ASCVD、心衰及房颤风险升高相关，且独立于平均 BMI；糖尿病可能通过代谢灵活性受损与治疗相关体重波动放大上述通路；糖尿病特异性队列提示，结构化的有意减重可能减弱非结构化体重循环（weight cycling）所伴随的不良关联。

Left Bundle Branch Area Pacing Improves Atrial Function and Reverses Atrial Remodeling in Dyssynchrony Heart Failure Model

J Am Heart Assoc·2026-10-10·PMID 42857974
房颤与消融 Journal Article

Weng S、Pei J、Li H 等 25 位作者

英文摘要

Background Left bundle branch area pacing may reduce new-onset atrial fibrillation. However, its mechanism and the comparison of atrial reverse remodeling effect between left bundle branch pacing (LBBP) and left ventricular septal pacing (LVSP) has not been elucidated. We aim to investigate reverse atrial remodeling by LBBP and LVSP in a canine model of rapid pacing-induced dyssynchronous heart failure (DHF).Methods Twenty-six beagles followed a 6-week protocol: controls (n=4), rapid atrial pacing (n=4), DHF (n=6), DHF+LBBP and DHF+LVSP (3 weeks DHF followed by 3 weeks LBBP or LVSP, both n=6). Atrial emptying fraction, functional, and electrophysiological parameters were dynamically assessed. Atrial fibrosis was analyzed in tissue samples.Results Within 3 weeks DHF increased atrial volumes and diminished RA and LA emptying fraction and strains significantly compared with baseline. Subsequent 3 weeks of LBBP increased emptying fraction and strains significantly. At week 6, P-wave duration was shorter in DHF+LBBP than in DHF. Atrial fibrillation was only inducible in DHF animals. Left atrial appendage levels of collagen I/III and α-SMA deposition and fibrosis were lower in DHF+LBBP compared with DHF animals. LBBP reversed atrial structural and electrical parameters to the levels in rapid atrial pacing animals. LVSP improved those parameters compared with those in DHF but did not normalize them to the levels in the rapid atrial pacing animals.Conclusions LBBP significantly reverses DHF-induced functional, cellular, and molecular remodeling in the atria, while a smaller amelioration is achieved with LVSP. The findings may explain the reduction in new-onset atrial fibrillation after LBBP.

与基线相比，3 周 DHF 即显著增大心房容积、降低左右心房（RA、LA）排空分数与应变；随后 3 周 LBBP 显著提高排空分数与应变。第 6 周时 DHF+LBBP 组 P 波时限短于 DHF 组，AF 仅在 DHF 组可被诱发。左心耳 I/III 型胶原、α-SMA 沉积及纤维化程度在 DHF+LBBP 组低于 DHF 组。LBBP 使心房结构及电学参数恢复至单纯快速心房起搏组水平；LVSP 虽较 DHF 组改善上述参数，但未能恢复至快速心房起搏组水平。摘要未报告具体效应量、95%CI 与 P 值。

在犬 DHF 模型中，LBBP 可显著逆转心房功能性、细胞及分子层面的重构，而 LVSP 仅带来较小幅度改善；该结果可能解释 LBBP 后新发 AF 减少的机制，并为伴失同步的心衰患者选择起搏方式提供房性获益方面的实验依据。局限性在于：此为小样本动物模型、随访仅 3 周、缺乏具体统计量与临床终点，结论外推至人群需进一步临床研究验证。

Left Atrial Enlargement Identifies Risk of Stroke Independent of Atrial Fibrillation

J Am Heart Assoc·2026-10-10·PMID 42858036
房颤与消融 Journal Article

Loft JJ、Kühl JT、Pham MHC 等 10 位作者

英文摘要

Background Left atrial (LA) enlargement has been proposed to increase stroke risk independent of atrial fibrillation (AF), but evidence accounting for AF over time is limited. We tested whether the association between LA enlargement and stroke persist independent of new-onset AF.Methods In this observational study, participants from the Copenhagen General Population Study underwent cardiac computed tomography from 2010 through 2018 and were followed until December 2022. LA volume was indexed to body surface area. LA enlargement was defined using sex-specific cutoffs. The primary outcome was incident stroke. Secondary outcomes were incident AF, heart failure, dementia, and all-cause mortality. Individuals with baseline AF were excluded. Hazard ratios (HRs) were estimated using cause-specific Cox models with AF modeled as a time-varying covariate.Results Among 10 024 individuals (mean age 60 years, 57% women), 684 (6.8%) had LA enlargement. Over a median follow-up of 7.3 years stroke occurred in 356 individuals (3.6%). LA enlargement was associated with a higher absolute risk of stroke (11.1% versus 4.6%, risk difference 6.5% [95% CI, 2.4-10.6]) and an increased hazard (adjusted HR, 1.49 [95% CI, 1.09-2.05]; P=0.013), which remained similar after adjustment for incident AF, and in separate models using the CHA2DS2-VA score and P-wave terminal force in lead V1. LA enlargement was also associated with higher risk of AF (HR, 3.77 [95% CI, 3.00-4.73]) and heart failure (HR, 3.59 [95% CI, 2.54-5.06]) but not with dementia or all-cause mortality.Conclusions LA enlargement was associated with incident stroke independent of incident AF, supporting LA enlargement as a marker of thromboembolic risk.

左房扩大是否独立于AF增加卒中风险，是长期存在争议的问题；既往证据多未在随访中动态校正新发AF，难以区分左房扩大本身的血栓栓塞效应与AF这一中间因素的介导作用，因此需要在大样本、长期随访人群中检验校正时间依赖性AF后相关性是否仍然存在。

方法

基于Copenhagen General Population Study的观察性队列研究。2010—2018年接受心脏计算机断层扫描（CT）者纳入，随访至2022年12月。左房容积按体表面积标化，左房扩大采用性别特异性界值定义。排除基线AF者。主要终点为新发卒中；次要终点为新发AF、心力衰竭、痴呆和全因死亡。采用病因特异性Cox模型估计风险比（HR），并将AF作为时间依赖性协变量建模；另在分别纳入CHA2DS2-VA评分和V1导联P波终末电势的模型中重复分析。

结果

结论与意义

在无基线AF的普通人群中，左房扩大与新发卒中风险独立相关，且不能用随访期间新发AF解释，支持将左房扩大作为血栓栓塞风险的独立标志物，提示仅以AF有无决定抗凝策略可能不足。局限性包括观察性设计、左房容积为单次CT测量、人群以丹麦白人为主、卒中亚型及抗凝治疗情况摘要未报告，CHA2DS2-VA评分等模型中的具体效应值亦未报告，尚需外部队列验证并评估以左房扩大指导抗凝决策的获益与风险。

First-in-human experience and one-year outcomes using a novel conformal pulsed-field ablation balloon catheter for pulmonary vein isolation in paroxysmal atrial fibrillation

J Interv Card Electrophysiol·2026-10-10·PMID 42859373
房颤与消融 Journal Article

Xiong S、Luo D、Luo Y 等 15 位作者

英文摘要

Background Thermal balloon catheters have improved the efficiency of pulmonary vein isolation (PVI) in atrial fibrillation (AF). Reports assessing additional safety and effectiveness of pulsed field ablation (PFA) balloon catheters for PVI are limited. This study aims to assess the safety and effectiveness of a conformal PFA balloon catheter for PVI in paroxysmal AF.Methods 35 patients with paroxysmal AF (PAF) were treated with a PFBalloon catheter (Biphasic, Bipolar, 750 V). The balloon was inflated with 10:1 saline/contrast, with volume adjusted for spherical wide-antral or pear-shaped ostial-junction PV ablation to ensure optimal tissue contact. The primary safety endpoint was absence of periprocedural primary adverse events. The primary effectiveness endpoint was acute PVI. The secondary effectiveness endpoint included durable PVI assessed by remapping at three months post-ablation and 1-year clinical outcomes.Results All targeted PVs (142/142) in 35 patients were acutely isolated, with 32.3 ± 4.5 applications per patient and 7.9 ± 1.0 per vein. Procedure and left atrial dwell times were 133 ± 46 and 56 ± 25 min, respectively. No hemolysis-related acute kidney injury or severe device-related complications occurred. PVI durability was 76.0% (19/25) per patient and 93.1% (95/102) per vein. After a median follow-up of 367 (interquartile range 357-380) days, 1-year Kaplan-Meier estimate for freedom from atrial arrhythmia (AF/atrial flutter/atrial tachycardia) ≥30 s was 90.7% (95% CI, 81.2%-100%).Conclusions The conformal PFBalloon catheter demonstrated favorable safety, efficiency, and effectiveness for PVI in PAF patients, achieving durable lesions and a high rate of freedom from atrial arrhythmias recurrence.Trial registration clinicaltrials.gov NCT07320976.

Cardiometabolic health-frailty phenotypes and bidirectional transitions between atrial fibrillation and heart failure: multistate trajectories and proteomic signatures

J Nutr Health Aging·2026-10-10·PMID 42858504
房颤与消融 Journal Article

Cui C、Liu B、Zhang G 等 5 位作者

英文摘要

Objective To examine whether a joint cardiometabolic health-frailty phenotype is associated with incident atrial fibrillation (AF), incident heart failure (HF) and progression to AF-HF comorbidity, and to identify candidate circulating protein signals related to this phenotype.Methods Among 319,048 UK Biobank participants without AF or HF, cardiometabolic status (healthy [CMH] or unhealthy [CMU]) and Frailty status (non-frail [NF] or pre-frail/frail [F]) defined four phenotypes. Cox and clock-forward multistate models estimated disease-specific and transition-specific hazards. Proteomic feature selection and exploratory mediation analyses included 28,206 participants with Olink plasma proteomic data.Results During follow-up, 17,844 participants developed AF, 5,410 developed HF, and 2,403 and 791 progressed from AF and HF, respectively, to comorbidity. Compared with CMH-NF, CMU-F was associated with the greatest risks of incident HF (hazard ratio (HR), 2.76; 95% confidence interval (CI), 2.59-2.95) and AF (HR, 1.49; 95% CI, 1.43-1.55). Corresponding multistate HRs were 2.98 (95% CI, 2.76-3.22) for baseline-to-HF, 1.45 (1.39-1.51) for baseline-to-AF, 1.76 (1.57-1.98) for AF-to-comorbidity, and 1.27 (1.02-1.57) for HF-to-comorbidity. Positive additive interactions indicated excess risk from coexisting cardiometabolic unhealthiness and frailty. Thirteen proteins showed statistical mediation signals for both outcomes; FABP4 yielded the largest positive mediated proportions (AF, 49.8%; HF, 32.9%) and transition-specific signals for baseline-to-AF and AF-to-comorbidity.Conclusions Coexisting cardiometabolic unhealthiness and frailty identified individuals at greatest risk of AF, HF, and subsequent comorbidity. Exploratory proteomic analyses identified FABP4 as the most consistent preliminary signal, which warrants further validation in independent cohorts.

一句话结论

在31.9万例UK Biobank参与者中，代谢不健康合并衰弱（CMU-F）者发生AF、HF及进展为AF-HF共病的风险均最高，FABP4为最一致的候选蛋白信号。

研究动机

心脏代谢健康不良与衰弱常并存，但二者联合表型与房颤（AF）、心衰（HF）发病以及AF与HF相互转化的双向进程之间的关系尚不清楚，既往研究多分别考察代谢因素或衰弱，缺乏对多状态转归轨迹及潜在循环蛋白标志物的系统分析。

方法

英文摘要

Myotonic Dystrophy type 1 (DM1) is a multisystem disorder caused by CTG repeat expansions in the dystrophia myotonica protein kinase (DMPK) gene.
