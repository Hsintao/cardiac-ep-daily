Left Atrial Appendage Closure or Medical Therapy in Atrial Fibrillation: A Systematic Review and Meta-analysis.

Cardiology·2026-10-12·PMID 42862780
房颤与消融 Journal Article Review

Ahmad Al-Abdouh、Omar Obeidat、Khaled Alabdallah 等 15 位作者

英文摘要

BACKGROUND: The use of anticoagulation in patients with atrial fibrillation is often limited by the significant risk of bleeding, and difficulties maintaining consistent adherence over time. These challenges have led to the development of left atrial appendage closure (LAAC) as an alternative stroke prevention strategy in this patient population. Our study aimed to evaluate LAAC versus medical therapy in patients with atrial fibrillation. METHODS: A comprehensive search was conducted using PubMed, Embase, and Cochrane Library from database inception through April 1, 2026. The predefined endpoints were composite outcome of systemic embolism, cardiovascular mortality, or stroke in addition to other endpoints including all-cause mortality, cardiovascular mortality, strokes, and major bleeding. All outcomes were pooled using a random effect Mantel-Haenszel model. The between-study variance (τ2) was estimated using the Paul Mendel method, which is the preferred method when the number of included studies is small. RESULTS: A total of five randomized controlled trials, 5,404 patients were analyzed; 2,878 underwent LAAC and 2,526 received medical therapy. The median follow-up across studies was 4 years. LAAC met noninferiority compared with medical therapy in atrial fibrillation for major bleeding (RR 0.86; 95% CI 0.59-1.26) and all-cause mortality (RR 0.93; 95% CI 0.71-1.22). In contrast, noninferiority was not met for the composite outcome (RR 0.97; 95% CI 0.64-1.47), cardiovascular mortality (RR 0.86; 95% CI 0.51-1.42), systemic embolism (RR 1.93; 95% CI 0.21-17.5), and all stroke (RR 1.11; 95% CI 0.73-1.67). CONCLUSION: This meta-analysis showed that LAAC was noninferior to anticoagulation for all-cause mortality and major bleeding, but it did not meet noninferiority for the other assessed outcomes, including stroke, cardiovascular mortality, and/or systemic embolism.

Tricuspid Stenosis Due to Cardiac Sarcoma.

Cureus·2026-10-12·PMID 42861164
房颤与消融 Case Reports Journal Article

Mirza M Baig、Muhammad Ansar

英文摘要

Primary cardiac sarcomas are rare, aggressive malignant tumors with high recurrence rates and poor prognosis. Their initial presentation is often vague, contributing to diagnostic delay and rapid clinical deterioration. We report the case of a 32-year-old man who presented with gastrointestinal symptoms and constitutional features, initially suggesting a hepatobiliary or infectious cause. As his condition progressed, signs of right-sided heart failure emerged, prompting transthoracic echocardiography, which revealed a large right atrial mass causing tricuspid inflow obstruction and a significant pericardial effusion. Surgical resection confirmed biphasic synovial sarcoma, a subtype previously described in small case series. Despite an initially uncomplicated postoperative course, he subsequently developed atrial flutter with associated left ventricular dysfunction, necessitating cardioversion. Anthracycline toxicity remains a possible contributing factor. This case underscores the importance of maintaining a broad differential diagnosis in young patients with unexplained systemic symptoms and highlights the diagnostic value of early echocardiographic assessment when right-sided cardiac involvement is suspected. It also reinforces the need for multidisciplinary management given the tumor's aggressive behavior and potential for rapid clinical decline.

Physical activity, atrial fibrillation and the dose-response relationship: a systematic review and meta-analysis.

Eur J Prev Cardiol·2026-10-12·PMID 42861574
房颤与消融 Journal Article

C L Davies、S Pallikadavath、D S March 等 8 位作者

英文摘要

BACKGROUND AND AIM: Regular physical activity (PA) may reduce atrial fibrillation (AF) risk in the general population, however, contemporary data suggests individuals engaging in the highest levels of PA may exhibit a paradoxical rise in AF risk. The point at which the dose-response curve reverses remains unclear. This meta-analysis aimed to assess the association between PA and AF across the full continuum of PA exposure. METHODS: Four databases were searched (from January 1990 to June 2025). Eligible studies included those that reported PA exposure compared against a sedentary control group and assessed AF incidence or prevalence. PA exposures were standardised to metabolic equivalent of task hours per week (MET-hr/wk). Pooled odds ratios (ORs) were calculated using a weighted random-effects model. A weighted restricted cubic spline model of logarithmic odds was used to produce a dose-response curve. RESULTS: 37 studies were included (n = 1,298,291 individuals). In the general population, PA was associated with a reduction in odds of AF by 22% (22 studies; OR 0.78; 95% CI 0.68, 0.89). Athlete status was associated with an increase in the odds of AF (15 studies; OR 2.74; 95% CI 1.72, 4.39). Dose-response analysis (26 studies; n = 1,022,790 individuals) showed 26 MET-hr/wk of PA was associated with the greatest reduction in odds of AF, with doses beyond 78 MET-hr/wk associated with increased odds of AF; the equivalent of 6-7 hours per week of endurance training. CONCLUSION: PA and AF demonstrate a U-shaped relationship whereby beyond a quantitative dose threshold of 78 MET-hr/wk, the protective effect of exercise may reverse.

Blanking Period after Atrial Fibrillation Catheter Ablation: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis.

Pacing Clin Electrophysiol·2026-10-12·PMID 42860654
房颤与消融 Journal Article Review

David T Zhang、Sakshum Chadha、Suraj S Patel 等 8 位作者

英文摘要

BACKGROUND: Atrial arrhythmias that recur within the first 3 months after atrial fibrillation (AF) catheter ablation are conventionally attributed to a "blanking period" and not classified as procedural failure. Accumulating evidence suggests that such early recurrences of atrial tachyarrhythmias (ERAT) may instead predict late recurrence, raising concern that the conventional 3-month window overestimates the duration during which recurrences are benign. OBJECTIVE: To assess the association between ERAT during each of the first 3 months after AF catheter ablation and late recurrence of atrial tachyarrhythmias (LRAT), and whether this association differs by ablation energy source. METHODS: We searched Ovid MEDLINE, Embase, and Web of Science without restriction on date or publication status for studies of AF catheter ablation reporting ERAT during defined intervals of the blanking period, stratified by subsequent LRAT status. Ablation energy sources included radiofrequency, cryoballoon, and pulsed field. The exposure was ERAT during the first, second, and third months after ablation, and the outcome was LRAT. Odds ratios (ORs) with 95% confidence intervals (CIs) were pooled using random-effects models. RESULTS: Twelve studies comprising 7,751 patients (2,505 with LRAT, 5,246 without) were included. Mean follow-up was 22.0 months, mean age 60.7 years, 25.8% were women, 52.5% had paroxysmal AF, mean CHA2DS2-VASc score was 2.1, mean left atrial diameter 42.6 mm, and mean left ventricular ejection fraction 58.1%. ERAT was associated with subsequent LRAT, and the strength of this association increased across the blanking period: month 1 (12 studies; OR 1.76, 95% CI 1.01-3.08; p = 0.05), month 2 (10 studies; OR 3.46, 95% CI 2.15-5.57; p < 0.001), and month 3 (10 studies; OR 21.4, 95% CI 10.9-42.2; p < 0.001). The graded association was reproduced within the radiofrequency and cryoballoon subgroups. CONCLUSION: ERAT was associated with subsequent LRAT throughout the blanking period, with a graded relationship that strengthened month by month and was strongest in the third month. These findings support a blanking period shorter than the conventional 3 months.

结论与意义

整个空白期内ERAT均与后续LRAT相关，且呈逐月增强的梯度关系，第3个月最强：提示越接近传统3个月界限的复发，越难视为良性。结果支持将空白期缩短至短于现行3个月；这对早期复发后的再消融决策、抗心律失常药物停用时机和随访安排有直接影响。局限性：研究为观察性数据的汇总，ERAT监测方式（如间断holter与植入式监测）与复发定义可能异质；脉冲场消融证据不足；OR随时间递增可能部分反映随访窗口与检出强度的差异，尚需前瞻性研究验证缩短空白期后的临床结局。

Association Between Baseline Cognitive Impairment and Atrial Fibrillation Recurrence After Catheter Ablation

Pacing Clin Electrophysiol·2026-10-12·PMID 42864730
房颤与消融 Journal Article

Dong H、Ying Q、Zhou L 等 7 位作者

英文摘要

Background Cognitive impairment is common among patients with atrial fibrillation (AF), yet its prognostic significance in the context of catheter ablation remains unclear.Aims We aimed to investigate whether baseline cognitive impairment, assessed by the Montreal Cognitive Assessment (MoCA), was associated with post-ablation AF recurrence.Methods Based on the China-AF Registry, we identified 2692 patients with nonvalvular AF who underwent index catheter ablation and had baseline MoCA assessments from 2022 to 2024. Patients were stratified into mild cognitive impairment (MCI, MoCA ≤ 26) and normal cognition (MoCA > 26) groups. The primary outcome was AF recurrence beyond the blanking period. The secondary outcome was a composite of all-cause death, ischemic stroke, and major bleeding. To adjust for confounding factors, we applied inverse probability of treatment weighting (IPTW) methodology. Weighted Cox proportional hazards models were used to estimate adjusted hazard ratios (aHRs).Results After IPTW adjustment, all baseline covariates were well balanced (all standardized mean differences < 0.03). Over a median follow-up of 218 days (IQR, 192-376), the MCI group had a significantly higher risk of AF recurrence (aHR 1.48; 95% CI, 1.27-1.73; p < 0.001). The composite secondary endpoint did not demonstrate a statistically significant between-group difference (aHR, 1.05; 95% CI, 0.25-4.52; p = 0.945). The association between baseline MCI and AF recurrence was consistent across all prespecified subgroups (all p for interaction > 0.05).Conclusion In this large prospective registry study, an independent association was observed between baseline cognitive impairment and an elevated risk of post-ablation AF recurrence. Cognitive assessment may be useful for risk stratification but requires external validation.Clinical trial registration Clinicaltrials.gov Identifier: NCT06987825.

方法

基于中国房颤登记（China-AF Registry）的前瞻性队列研究，纳入2022—2024年接受首次导管消融、且具备基线MoCA评估的非瓣膜性AF患者2692例，按MoCA≤26（轻度认知障碍，MCI）与MoCA>26（认知正常）分组。主要终点为空白期后的AF复发；次要终点为全因死亡、缺血性卒中与大出血的复合终点。采用逆概率治疗加权（IPTW）校正混杂，并以加权Cox比例风险模型估算校正风险比（aHR），同时进行预设亚组交互检验。

结果
