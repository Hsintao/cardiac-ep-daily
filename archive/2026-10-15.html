英文摘要

Percutaneous left atrial appendage closure is now an established strategy for thromboembolic prevention in atrial fibrillation. However, the organizational requirements governing its performance remain heterogeneous across regulations and healthcare systems, particularly in France. In this context, it is relevant to assess whether, in experienced centers, an organizational model based on specific expertise and a rapid surgical referral pathway could provide a safe alternative to the presence of on-site cardiac surgery. This article is in favor of extension in France of percutaneous left atrial appendage closure in centres without on-site cardiac surgery.

SGLT2 Inhibitor Therapy and Risk of Incident Atrial Fibrillation in Patients With HFpEF and Type 2 Diabetes.

Cardiology·2026-10-15·PMID 42879337
房颤与消融 Journal Article

Irene Animah Acheampong、George Blankson、Dennis Danso Kumi 等 7 位作者

英文摘要

Background Atrial fibrillation (AF) frequently coexists with Heart Failure with Preserved Ejection Fraction, particularly in patients with Type 2 Diabetes, due to shared cardiometabolic and inflammatory mechanisms. Sodium-glucose cotransporter-2 inhibitors (SGLT2i) have demonstrated cardiovascular benefit in HFpEF, but data regarding prevention of incident AF remain limited. Objectives To evaluate the association between SGLT2 inhibitor therapy and incident atrial fibrillation/flutter in patients with HFpEF and diabetes mellitus. Methods We performed a retrospective propensity score-matched cohort study using the TriNetX Research Network. Adults aged ≥18 years with HFpEF and diabetes mellitus receiving oral antihyperglycemic therapy were identified. Patients receiving SGLT2 inhibitors were matched 1:1 with those receiving Dipeptidyl peptidase-4 inhibitors. The primary outcome was incident AF/flutter. Secondary outcomes included all-cause mortality, ischemic stroke, and composite major adverse cardiovascular events (cMACE; all-cause mortality, ischemic stroke, or acute myocardial infarction). Associations were assessed using hazard ratios (HRs) and risk ratios (RRs). Results After propensity matching, 37,108 patients were included (18,554 per group). Compared with DPP4 inhibitor therapy, SGLT2 inhibitor use was associated with lower incidence of AF/flutter (17.8% vs 19.2%; HR 0.84, RR 0.93), all-cause mortality (12.5% vs 21.4%; HR 0.53, RR 0.59), ischemic stroke (7.4% vs 8.7%; HR 0.78, RR 0.85), and cMACE (24.4% vs 33.0%; HR 0.66, RR 0.74). Conclusions In patients with HFpEF and diabetes mellitus, SGLT2 inhibitor therapy was associated with lower rates of incident AF/flutter and adverse cardiovascular outcomes, supporting a potential role for SGLT2 inhibitors in modifying arrhythmic risk in addition to established cardiometabolic benefits.

Graph neural network for predicting reentrant drivers in atrial digital twins.

Comput Methods Programs Biomed·2026-10-15·PMID 42879882
房颤与消融 Journal Article

Shane Loeffler、Yash Lal、Alana Yee 等 10 位作者

英文摘要

BACKGROUND AND OBJECTIVE: Atrial Fibrillation (AF) is the most common cardiac arrhythmia and is associated with high risk of stroke and heart failure. While pulmonary vein isolation is a standard treatment, its efficacy is suboptimal for patients with persistent AF (PsAF), resulting in high recurrence rates. Increasing evidence indicates that fibrotic remodeling can create arrhythmogenic substrates outside the pulmonary veins, which may contribute to recurrence. Previous works have shown that PsAF Digital Twins (DT) can locate these arrhythmogenic regions and inform ablation targets for AF treatment, characterizing the full arrhythmogenic substrate through iterative simulations, including emergent reentries. While PsAF DTs are effective, their use in clinical practice is constrained by substantial computational demands. METHODS: In this study, we developed a Graph Neural Network (GNN) to directly identify regions sustaining reentrant drivers (ablation targets) without the need of computationally expensive simulations. The GNN was trained on 31 high-resolution PsAF DTs. Afterwards, the GNN's predictions were prospectively validated on four new PsAF DTs, where the predictions served as virtual ablation targets and new simulations were performed to evaluate the GNN's predictions to eliminate reentrant drivers on new geometries and fibrotic distributions. RESULTS: Across 8-fold cross validation, the GNN achieved an AUROC of 0.884 and an AUPRC of 0.174, both of which surpassed all other tested models. GNN's positive predictions with the greatest confidence were refined to regions with high fibrotic density and moderate fibrotic entropy. In the proof-of-concept prospective validation, GNN-guided ablation reduced emergent reentries by over 70% relative to the wide antral circumferential ablation pulmonary vein isolation (WACA PVI)-only control and reduced the mean number of additional simulation rounds required to reach non-inducibility by 66%. CONCLUSION: This study demonstrates the potential of a GNN to guide DT-based ablation for PsAF patients and to reduce the iterative simulation burden of DT workflows. These findings support the feasibility of GNN-guided substrate prioritization, with further validation supporting clinical integration.

GNN可在不依赖高计算成本仿真的前提下优先排序致心律失常基质靶区，并显著减轻数字孪生流程的迭代仿真负担，为PsAF基质消融的靶点规划提供了可行的计算辅助路径。局限性包括：训练与验证样本量小（31例训练、4例验证）、验证终点为仿真环境下的虚拟消融与诱发性而非真实临床终点、缺乏组织学或标测金标准对照，尚需更大规模数据与真实临床研究支持其临床整合。

Machine-learning versus traditional scoring methods for prediction of atrial fibrillation recurrence after catheter ablation: A systematic review and meta-analysis.

Curr Probl Cardiol·2026-10-15·PMID 42880521
房颤与消融 Journal Article Review

Thomas Koh、Stefany Jeong、Edward Dababneh 等 6 位作者

英文摘要

BACKGROUND: Atrial fibrillation (AF) recurrence after catheter ablation remains common, with recurrence rates up to 45%. Traditional risk scores demonstrate only modest predictive performance. Machine learning (ML) approaches have shown promise in improving risk stratification by leveraging high-dimensional clinical and imaging data, but direct comparisons with traditional scores remain limited. OBJECTIVE: To compare the discriminative performance of ML models versus traditional risk scores in predicting AF recurrence following catheter ablation. METHODS: MEDLINE, EMBASE, Web of Science, and Cochrane databases were searched to 11 June 2026 for studies reporting head-to-head comparisons of ML and traditional risk models predicting AF recurrence after ablation. The primary outcome was model discrimination, measured by the C-statistic. Random-effects meta-analysis was used to pool discrimination estimates, and meta-regression was performed to compare ML and traditional approaches. Subgroup analysis was conducted to explore the effect of ML model architecture. Methodological quality was assessed using the PROBAST tool. RESULTS: Eleven studies (n = 17,150) were included. ML models demonstrated higher pooled discrimination compared with traditional risk scores (AUC 0.80, 95% CI 0.72-0.86 vs. 0.58, 95% CI 0.53-0.62; QM 18.96, p < 0.001). Subgroup analysis suggested that multimodal fusion models achieved the highest pooled discrimination, followed by neural-network models. Tree-based models did not significantly outperform traditional risk scores in the primary analysis. CONCLUSIONS: ML models showed higher discrimination compared to traditional risk scores in predicting AF recurrence after ablation. Methodological limitations, lack of calibration reporting, and absence of external validation limit immediate clinical adoption.

Large-scale plasma metabolic profiling identifies signatures and biomarkers associated with air pollution and arrhythmias: A prospective cohort study from UK Biobank.

Ecotoxicol Environ Saf·2026-10-15·PMID 42880149
房颤与消融 Journal Article

Hongguang Wu、Nan He、Peiyao Li 等 14 位作者

英文摘要

BACKGROUND: Air pollution is an established modifiable risk factor for cardiac arrhythmia, yet the metabolic mechanisms underlying this association remain poorly understood. This study aimed to elucidate how air pollutants promote arrhythmias through metabolic pathway perturbations. METHODS: We conducted a prospective analysis of 449,068 UK Biobank participants without baseline arrhythmia. Exposures to PM2.5, PM10, NO2, and NOx were assessed using land use regression models. Plasma metabolomic profiling including 170 metabolites via 1H-NMR was performed at baseline. Elastic net regression, Cox proportional hazards models, and mediation analysis were employed to identify pollutant-associated metabolic signatures, quantify their mediating effects, and characterize pathway enrichment across different kinds of arrhythmias. RESULTS: During a median follow-up of 16.31 years, a total of 47,381 arrhythmia cases were identified. All four pollutants were prospectively associated with increased arrhythmia risk (hazard ratios ranged from 1.015 to 1.257 per 10 μg/m3). The derived metabolomic signatures significantly mediated these associations between different air pollutants and arrhythmia (mediation proportions: PM2.5-related, 11.19%; PM10-related, 5.19%; NO2-related, 7.44%; NOx-related, 9.82%). Pathway enrichment analysis consistently identified lipid metabolism, ketone body metabolism, and amino acid metabolism as core affected pathways. Notably, compared to atrial fibrillation and supraventricular tachycardia, the ketone body metabolic pathway appeared to account for a larger proportion of the association with intraventricular block. CONCLUSIONS: This study suggests that air pollution increases arrhythmia risk through distinct, subtype-specific metabolic perturbations, providing novel insights into the metabolic pathways linking environmental pollutants to different arrhythmia subtypes.

研究动机

空气污染是心律失常已确立的可干预危险因素，但其代谢机制尚不清楚。既往研究多聚焦单一污染物或单一终点（如 AF），缺少大样本前瞻性队列中代谢组学特征的中介效应量化，也缺乏对不同心律失常亚型间通路差异的比较，本研究拟通过血浆代谢谱解析污染物促心律失常的代谢通路扰动。

方法

前瞻性队列分析，纳入 UK Biobank 中 449,068 名基线无心律失常的参与者。采用土地利用回归模型评估 PM2.5、PM10、NO2、NOx 暴露；基线以 1H-NMR 检测 170 种血浆代谢物。统计学方法包括弹性网络回归构建污染物相关代谢特征、Cox 比例风险模型估计污染物与心律失常风险的关联，以及中介分析量化代谢特征的中介比例，并按心律失常亚型（包括 AF、室上性心动过速、室内传导阻滞等）行通路富集分析。摘要未报告协变量调整的具体方案及终点判定方式。

结果

中位随访 16.31 年期间共识别 47,381 例新发心律失常。四种污染物均与心律失常风险升高呈前瞻性关联，每 10 μg/m³ 的 HR 范围为 1.015–1.257。所构建的代谢组学特征对不同污染物与心律失常的关联均具显著中介作用，中介比例分别为 PM2.5 11.19%、PM10 5.19%、NO2 7.44%、NOx 9.82%。通路富集分析一致提示脂质代谢、酮体代谢和氨基酸代谢为核心受累通路。与 AF 和室上性心动过速相比，酮体代谢通路在室内传导阻滞中所占的关联比例更大。摘要未报告各 HR 的 95%CI 与 P 值。

结论与意义

Left bundle branch area pacing (LBBAP) has emerged as a physiological pacing strategy capable of restoring ventricular synchrony in patients requiring permanent pacing or cardiac resynchronization therapy (CRT).
